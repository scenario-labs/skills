## Live measurements

Fifteen cases run on After Effects 27.0 (build 27.0x78) on macOS through the
scenario-after-effects-expert bridge: CSV and ES3 probes, `duplicate()` semantics, render queue API,
the end-to-end versioning job (two layout rules), `aerender` whole-queue and single-comp modes, undo
nesting, paragraph overflow, error messages, MOGRT export, Essential Properties overrides, preflight
with fonts and renderer, data validation, the saved queue after `aerender`, resume from decoded
files, the per-row status file. Results are cited in procedures.md as `live 27.0`.
