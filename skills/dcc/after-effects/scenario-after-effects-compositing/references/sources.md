## Live measurements

Every `checked: live 27.0` block was run on After Effects 27.0 (build 27.0x78) on macOS through the
bridge, against a synthetic green screen plate with a known truth matte (hair strands, a 45 % veil,
a motion-blurred edge, a spilled white shirt, a light stand). The numbers in the skill come from
those runs.
