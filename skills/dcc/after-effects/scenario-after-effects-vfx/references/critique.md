# Critique

Check every effect shot against these gates before showing it. A failed measurable gate blocks
delivery. A failed visual gate goes back to the stage named, with the change listed under "Do
instead".

## Measurable gates

| Check                     | Pass                                                                                                                            | How                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bit depth                 | 16 bpc (32 only in a person's OCIO project, amounts redone)                                                                     | `app.project.bitsPerChannel`                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Rig clipping              | 0 px at 255 in all three channels on 8 frames                                                                                   | count pixels with R, G and B at 255 on the rig-only render                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Shot clipping             | under 1 % of pixels clipped after integration                                                                                   | same count on the final frames                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Renderer match            | GPU and software frames identical on one peak frame                                                                             | render the frame twice, difference 0                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Light level               | each element's peak within 5 % of the level planned                                                                             | read the peak of the element alone in the light comp                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Particle counts           | within 15 % of the on-screen target                                                                                             | count blobs on one frame                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Particle sizes            | measured diameter within 1 px; no size under the visible minimum                                                                | `AEX.pn.sparks` warnings empty                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Loop                      | frame L against frame 0: difference 0                                                                                           | pixel difference                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Rays                      | foreground pixels and the 5th percentile lifted by 0                                                                            | difference against the frame without rays                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Polar ring                | seam score under 0.5 (luma step across 12 o'clock over the median step across other radial lines); round at r1                  | profile across the seam; frame at the end radius                                                                                                                                                                           |

| Element counts            | layers, streaks and rings returned by the job match the plan                                                                    | the job's return value                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Grain                     | std within 10 % of the plate's                                                                                                  | frame-to-frame std of flat patches; on a held frame, high-pass std of a flat patch before the CA (channel-scale CA lowers it: 0.75 to 0.40 levels)                                                                         |
| Decode                    | plate numbers read as After Effects reads them                                                                                  | ffmpeg's default H.264 decode reads 2.0 levels darker than After Effects; use `scale=in_range=tv:out_range=pc:flags=accurate_rnd+full_chroma_int` (0.13 levels off) or a frame rendered by After Effects                   |

## Visual gates

One contact sheet per shot: start, peak and end, over the plate, read at viewing size. The quality
bar has five checks, plus a sixth when a subject sits in the light.

| Gate                     | Pass                                                                                                    | Failure picture                                                                                                                              | Do instead                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hierarchy                | one element clearly dominates the frame                                                                 | every element equally bright: the eye has nowhere to land                                                                                    | cut secondary elements to half their light; keep the hero on the top tone                                                                                                    |
| Few large elements       | 3 to 6 large pieces carry the shape                                                                     | hundreds of small equal dots: the burst body reads as sand thrown in the air                                                                 | draw the body as procedural streaks and keep particles to 30 to 150 accents; move light into a few leaders and near pieces                                                   |
| Variation 5 to 10x       | size, speed and brightness spread by at least 5x                                                        | one size, one speed: a uniform sphere or an even snowfall                                                                                    | widen Size Variation, split into speed tiers (1, 0.6, 0.35) and depth planes                                                                                                 |
| Core with radial falloff | the hottest point sits inside a colored falloff, from the attack frame on                               | a flat cream disc or egg with a hard edge (the core burned to the top tone over its whole area); on a hit, a flat disc on the first 3 frames | budget the center across core, streaks, flash and sparks; lower the source level so only the center reaches the top tone; bloom total near 2.0, not above 3.0; rig in Screen |
| Subject in the light     | the subject keeps its colors, reads through the flash and has a thin rim on the edges facing the source | a cut-out: a dark contour around the subject, which sits flat in front of the glow                                                           | choke the holdout about 2 px instead of blurring it; add the rim and the light wrap from the BACK light, weighted toward the origin                                          |
| Air                      | dark gaps between streaks, wisps and particles                                                          | a gray veil over the frame: rays or bloom lifting the whole image, haze midtones raised                                                      | fewer particles, lower the wide bloom copies, rays from the light only, a radial falloff on the rays                                                                         |
