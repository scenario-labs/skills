# Critique rubric: pose and print parts

Use at every gate. Score each line pass, fix, or not applicable, and write the evidence next to it (a number from `zb_print` or `zb_pose`, or the review sheet and the tile you judged). A line without evidence is not a pass. Views: scenario-zbrush-expert `zb_review.review` (front, right, back, three-quarter, top) with MatCap Gray; add Polyframe for joints and flat color for silhouettes.

## 1. The pose (what Plouffe, Pavlovich and Munoz Gomez look at)

| Check                                                                                                         | Evidence                                                                                                                              | Fix                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stands steady: weight over the support, nothing reads as falling                                              | `zb_print.balance`: `stands` true, margin in mm; front and side tiles                                                                 | shift the pelvis over the support foot, widen the stance, or plan a base (doc: no floating characters)          |
| Gesture reads before detail: a clear line of action, silhouette readable from the main view and three-quarter | review sheet, silhouettes                                                                                                             | rotate the torso and pelvis against each other; open limbs away from the body in the main view                  |
| Pose built from the center outward, parents before children                                                   | the pose plan order, `rigid_delta` of the pelvis and chest                                                                            | re-pose the central mass first (Plouffe, Pavlovich)                                                             |
| Joint angles match the plan                                                                                   | `rigid_delta` per segment within 2 degrees [added]                                                                                    | re-run the rig step for that chain                                                                              |
| Nothing unintended moved (planted feet, base)                                                                 | `rigid_delta(...)["max_disp"]` on those vertices near 0                                                                               | mask or exclude them from the rig                                                                               |
| Plates, weapon, bolts moved as one body each                                                                  | `rigid_pieces`: `ok`, no piece `bent` (rms under 0.05 mm [added])                                                                     | rigid weights to one bone (route A), or keep the piece its own SubTool and carry it (P5)                        |
| The weapon sits where the hand took it                                                                        | `follows`: residual p95 under 0.5 mm [added], `part_rigid`; `interference` alone is not evidence (a prop left far away also scores 0) | keep it in the TPose mesh; else the carrier's whole transform (`rotate_subtool_matrix`, `placement_correction`) |
| Grip closed around the handle, nothing passing through (Hasbro re-checks the grip)                            | close-up tile; `interference` 0 when printed separately                                                                               | adjust the fingers; for a mold, fingers must not touch each other or the thumb                                  |
| The transfer was safe                                                                                         | `tpose_to_subtools` `order_check.same`, point counts; ZPR saved after TPoseMesh; `clean_project` ok                                   | reload the saved ZTL, pose again without Gizmo deformers                                                        |
| Lineup of a series reads as a family (heights, masses)                                                        | side by side sheet                                                                                                                    | balance heights; big scale gaps suit animation more than statues (Plouffe)                                      |

## 2. Deformation after posing (Munoz Gomez, Plouffe)

| Check                                                                 | Evidence                                                                                                                | Fix                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No stretched or overlapping polygons at joints                        | `stretch_report`: stretched share near the rest pose, spots listed; Polyframe tile                                      | blur the transition, Smooth and Move the seam, or re-pose at a lower level                                |
| The seam was evened while the rest was protected                      | the fix order in the log: seam smoothing (Corrective Smooth band, or Polish under the mask) before the mask was cleared | redo the seam from the pose layer (Munoz Gomez)                                                           |
| Volumes follow the pose: bent limbs bulge, stretched ones flatten     | compressed and stretched spots against the anatomy; three-quarter tile                                                  | re-volume on a new top-level layer (aimed strokes, Inflate on a built mask)                               |
| Primitives still perfect (discs, bolts, perfect cylinders, IMM parts) | close-ups                                                                                                               | re-place them on the bent surface instead of bending them                                                 |
| Cloth, body and armor do not interpenetrate where it shows            | `piece_interference` on the TPose export, interference spots; Polyframe fill colors (Hasbro)                            | Intersection Masker plus masked Inflate [verify], Move Topological stroke on the piece or in the rig plan |
| Folds follow the new pose                                             | three-quarter tile: tension from the stretched joint, compression on the inner bend [added]                             | re-sculpt on the fix layer, or hand the spot list over                                                    |
| Detail reads on the pose, not only in neutral                         | close-up at the bends with the pose layer on                                                                            | refine on a pose-only layer: layers blend linearly (Munoz Gomez)                                          |
| Detail survived the transfer on every level                           | point counts from `tpose_to_subtools`; top-level close-up                                                               | redo the transfer from the saved ZTL; check the layer interpolation (Munoz Gomez)                         |
| Hidden or shadowed areas got only the time they need                  | where the hours went                                                                                                    | stop polishing what the final view hides (Plouffe)                                                        |

## 3. Print parts (Gaboury, Hasbro, Carratala, Maxon docs)

| Check                                                            | Evidence                                                                                                                     | Fix                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Size in millimeters matches the brief                            | `check_scale`: height error within 0.5 % [added]; one export scale on every part; `caliper` on any dimension the brief names | `set_export_scale` again; a part appended from another ZTool is suspect                                            |
| Nothing clips out of the brief box                               | `envelope` ok (overflow 0 per axis); one tile with the box shown (Gaboury)                                                   | re-pose the limb that pokes out, or ask for the size change and rescale in one call                                |
| Every part is closed, oriented outward, manifold                 | `print_report` watertight and volume gates; `is_polymesh3d_solid`                                                            | Close Holes, DynaMesh the part, or Live Boolean union; fix flipped faces                                           |
| One shell per part unless a loose piece is intended              | `shells`: floating solids 0                                                                                                  | merge it or make it its own part                                                                                   |
| Walls at or above the process minimum                            | `thickness`: thin share, p01, thin spots; From Thickness render on a copy                                                    | move the cutter inward (higher Thickness, or shrink the inner shell), or keep that end solid (Gaboury)             |
| Hollowing and wall fixes left the outside alone                  | `outer_drift` p99 within 0.02 mm [added] against the solid export                                                            | the fix went outside: redo it on the cutter (Hasbro: "don't change it from the outside")                           |
| Hollowed the Gaboury way                                         | a light copy shelled, inner wall kept and polished, subtracted from the untouched detailed part; drain and vents as cutters  | never shell the heavy sculpt; never print the 128 copy when detail matters                                         |
| Smallest features survive: edges, blade tips, fingers, strands   | thin spots at tips; close-ups at real size                                                                                   | edges at least 0.5 mm, strands fused or bunched (Hasbro); tips may taper where the design allows                   |
| Hollow parts drain and vent                                      | `shells`: sealed cavities 0 for resin; the slicer's layer preview for cups                                                   | drain at the base (insert touching the surface), vents at cup tops (Gaboury)                                       |
| Cuts sit at natural breaks and hide the seam                     | part list against costume seams, joints, the weapon at the hand                                                              | move the cut to a seam, collar, belt or cuff (Carratala, Hasbro)                                                   |
| Keys fit, align and hold                                         | `clearance`: median at target, no interference; `plan_keys` ok; two keys or a non-round key where spin matters               | resize the socket from the measured gap; add the second key; mushroom head or slot for retention (Hasbro, Gaboury) |
| Parts fit the printer                                            | `fits_volume` per part                                                                                                       | split again at another break                                                                                       |
| Polycount fits the slicer, no visible faceting                   | faces per part; `deviation` p99 within the printer's XY resolution [added]; close-ups before and after                       | raise that part's target; decimate SubTools with detail separately                                                 |
| Decimation kept density where detail matters                     | per-SubTool budgets or a mask (face, hands) set before Pre-process; pre-processed after the last edit                        | mask, pre-process again, decimate again (doc: later edits are ignored)                                             |
| Detail sized to the print                                        | a tile at the figure's real size on screen: nothing under the process floor that is meant to show                            | backfill, fuse or leave it to paint (Hasbro); stop detailing what the final view hides (Plouffe, Carratala)        |
| One key pair test-printed before the kit                         | the physical fit noted in the report                                                                                         | adjust the clearance from the print (Gaboury)                                                                      |
| Undercuts only matter for molds: parting line clean when molding | Group Front from the mold view, Draw Draft Analysis (Hasbro, doc)                                                            | add draft, split the part, separate touching fingers                                                               |

## 4. Delivery

| Check                                                                | Evidence                                                    | Fix                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Unit stated in the files and the note                                | STL header, 3MF `unit="millimeter"`, delivery note          | write it; STL itself carries none (doc)           |
| Each file named after its part, one file per part                    | `export_parts` names, report part list                      | re-export with names                              |
| The report says what was measured and what was only looked at        | `print_report` JSON (`failed`, `to_look_at`), review sheets | complete the missing check or say it was not done |
| Sources kept: posed ZTL with layers, print ZTL with booleans unbaked | versioned ZTL paths                                         | save them; never deliver from the only copy       |
