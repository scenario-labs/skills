## Other references used for maths [added]

- CIELAB conversion (sRGB D65, CIE 1976) and the just-noticeable difference of about 2.3 (Sharma, Digital Color Imaging Handbook, 2003) for zone thresholds.
- Immerkaer, "Fast Noise Variance Estimation" (Computer Vision and Image Understanding, 1996) for `noise_sigma`.
- W3C Compositing and Blending Level 1 for the soft light and overlay formulas in `blend`.
