## Sir Wade Neistadt (ex-DreamWorks production trainer)

**Workflows** (GMTet6nd_iM, 2021)

- No single workflow; change it per shot and hide it: "the workflow that you use should not be obvious to someone watching your shot" [00:07:02] [00:32:21].
- Seven workflows: pose to pose (thumbnail first, "it's faster to draw it than it is to pose it" [00:02:42]); straight ahead, usually in spline [00:08:27]; layered, one component finished before the next (ball: translate Y until perfect, then X) [00:11:36]; 2D sketch blocking [00:14:21]; reference blocking, poses every 2, 4, 5 or 7 frames [00:19:09]; extremes per body part and channel, keys scattered but organic [00:25:35] [00:27:45]; key categories in layered passes [00:28:37].
- Blocking is complete when all five key categories exist: main keys, anticipations, overlap and follow-through (overshoots), breakdowns, holds [00:29:08] [00:31:48]. Holds are a category: the character must "get somewhere and stay there, act in the pose, give the audience time to breathe" [00:31:14].
- Even spacing of key poses is always wrong: 1, 10, 20 is floaty [00:05:24]. Keying the whole body on the same frames is the pose-to-pose tell [00:07:02].
- Reference: key the landmarks (contacts, takeoff, apex, landing, blinks, eye darts) to capture rhythm, then push [00:23:38]. Rotomated too closely, his own Hiccup shot "looked like mocap data" [00:21:30]. Style decides how far: stylized features push, Call of Duty pushes only for clarity, VFX stays close [00:20:25] [00:20:57].
- Rigs arrive neutral and symmetric: never act from the default pose; decide the asymmetric shape first [00:16:02] [00:17:07].
- Anticipation is optional in games: a jump may start on the button frame [00:30:10].

**Dialogue** (5cIxEZwZmS4, 2021)

- Break the line down on paper: phrases, emotional cues, then every sound with a hard rule: M closes at least one frame [00:08:49]; B and P are built-up pressure released [00:09:21]; O is a circle; J and CH compress with air [00:09:53].
- Components in order: jaw, corners in/out, lip shape, tongue [00:13:42]. The jaw alone ("muppet version") must read the rhythm [00:21:19]; it can go past neutral into compression on closures [00:20:16]; group sounds, do not flap on every syllable [00:21:52]; jaw reversals are flags to check [00:22:25].
- The blanket "two frames early" is not detailed enough: a sound made when the shape forms (M) differs from one made when it breaks (B, P, K, L) [00:15:30] [00:17:06]; B/P lips closed before the sound frame, opening on it [00:19:14].
- Corners follow the neighboring sounds more than the current one [00:23:31]; no width changes inside a word [00:30:34]. Never record slow mirror reference [00:25:11].
- Tongue: L raised early, released with a big one-frame spacing; "the tongue is a very very fast muscle, it does not do a lot of easing"; R floats mid-mouth [00:37:24] [00:39:33]. Skipping the tongue makes "half the words not make any sense" [00:36:18].
- Four polish steps: breath, connectivity and fleshiness (lips fight the jaw, the M peel), graphic appealing shapes, mouth flow across the face [00:41:27] to [00:45:40].

**Graph Editor** (TMSpauVphNs, 2022, Maya 2020.4 on screen)

- Set Key reshapes the curve around a new key; enable Set Key > Set key preserves curve shape, or insert keys [00:01:45] [00:02:18]. A keying command that changes neighboring curves is a workflow bug, not something to live with. [added] The agent inserts (`A.insert_key`) and proves it with `A.curve_diff` on sampled snapshots before and after: existing keys and the curve outside the new key's two segments unchanged (the body digest's "Set key damage" gate).
- Stacked view is "an essential tool", especially for cycles; absolute view squashes everything under one traveling control [00:02:48] [00:03:55]. [added] `curve_lanes_png` is the agent's stacked view.
- Weighted tangents: shape by weight, never re-point handles: collapse the contact weights, lengthen the apex weight for hang time; "you change the weight, you never ever need to change the direction" [00:06:57] [00:07:29] [00:08:00]. Ball in f_00582: contacts near frames 1, 24, 47, apex frame 12.
- [added math] A gravity parabola between contact and apex is a cubic Bezier with one-third handles, contact slope 2H/T and a flat apex; Wade's recipe adds hang beyond gravity. Stylized or light: use it. Heavy or realistic: no.
- Palette (f_00933): translates red, green, blue; rotates orange, yellow, cyan; scales pinks and violets; select curves by channel name across all controls so no curve is forgotten [00:11:15] [00:15:37].

**Heavy weight** (ZYKAMCZq2UI, 2024; captions read like a machine-translation round trip)

- Two numbers first: object weight and character strength [00:00:00] [00:09:03].
- Resistance before release, even for gods: "It's impossible to fully reveal a character's strength until you show what they're facing" [00:01:49]; a boulder lifted without effort reads as styrofoam [00:06:37].
- Weight is "this break in the flow, this delay in motion", acceleration from rest [00:03:05]; strength is fewer frames from resistance to full extension [00:05:00] [00:06:20].
- Throws: the object lags the hand; "if it moves with your hand the whole time, it's not that heavy" [00:05:48]. Punches: lean in, then a short A to B [00:08:04].
- Force from the core outward (back, shoulders, arms), never all on one key [00:07:39] [00:09:34]. Scale and interpenetration can sell compression [00:08:32]; locked animation can be helped by sound, shake, dust [00:07:09].

## Autodesk Maya 2027 Help and Epic (documentation)

- Tangents (animation__graph-editor-tangents-curves): Plateau keeps extremes on keys; Clamped fixes pelvis and foot slip in cycles; Stepped Next jumps early; Quaternion curves with clamped tangents interpolate stepped; filters (Euler, Key Reducer, Smooth Butterworth keeps min and max, Gaussian, Peak Removal, Resample, Simplify) work on time curves only; Enable Stepped Preview on the Time Slider.
- Layers (animation__animation-layers): Additive sums, Override replaces, Passthrough makes weight an opacity; booleans and enums always override; mute an empty Override layer before keying it; muted layers are locked by default; wrist stabilized with an Override layer and IK Blend 0, 0, 1 at frames 0, 20, 27 on linear keys; merges follow stack order.
- Cached Playback (animation__cached-playback): on by default, needs the Evaluation Manager; yellow means Safe Mode; motion blur, Trax, classic dynamics disable it; playblast remains the review medium.
- Ghosting and trails (animation__ghosting-motion-trails): skinned meshes need Cached Playback; no ghosts of simulation; trails editable only on keyframes; do not mix editor ghosts with layer or Time Editor ghosts.
- HumanIK (animation__humanik-retargeting): bake from the HumanIK window; a Control-rig layer overrides the bake; Match Source gives a large character tiny steps; Knee/Elbow Max Extension 50 damps snapping; Mass Center Compensation 80; custom rig mapping (IK effectors T and R, FK R only, up vectors translation only).
- Time Editor (animation__time-editor): clips, transitions, relocators on similar frames; never with animation layers; remap needs identical skeletons and bone lengths.
- Sequencer and playblast (animation__sequencer-playblast-2027): playblast defaults to the active view, the Time Slider range, scale 0.5; `-frame` for single frames; `-offScreen`; a playblast faster than the viewport means the viewport was not real time.
- Root motion (animation__unreal-root-motion, UE 5.8): off until enabled per asset; Z root motion ignored while Walking or Falling; extraction runs on the Game Thread.
