# Expert notes: scenario-maya-modeling

The depth behind SKILL.md: what each expert does, why, and when their advice applies. Timestamps point into the source video; "frame" marks a contact-sheet observation from the project's notes. Source notes live in the project's `notes/modeling/` and `notes/fundamentals/` (never edited to fit this skill). [added] marks this skill's own reasoning.

## Mario Elementza: sub-D hard-surface topology (`_bpsEd_5IW4`, 2024)

Hard-surface and industrial-design modeling educator (Elementza courses; no studio credits found). The most complete reasoning source on where a cut goes, how dense to go, and when a triangle is acceptable.

**Purpose decides whether topology matters.** A still render from CAD or booleans: "this topic is not interesting for you" [00:00:32]. Games, animation, close hero renders, retopology of CAD: it does [01:36:02]. Background prop vs "full-blown in camera hero asset" changes every decision [01:00:27] [01:01:32].

**Bottom up.** Primary form with even quads first, secondary forms cut out of it [00:04:06]. If a region confuses you, duplicate, extract the simplest sub-piece and solve it alone; if the simple piece fails, the complex one will [00:05:13] [00:05:50]. Still stuck: model the form as if the cut did not exist, "as clean as a cube", then cut [00:15:07] [00:16:13].

**Even grid, never double.** "Minimal is fine as long as it's not double" [00:08:31]; past 2:1, balancing one side unbalances the other [00:07:59]. Close the largest possible quads first [00:06:55]; quads keep direction as loops are added, a triangle makes "edges flow everywhere" [00:07:28]. Stress test: keep adding loops; if it stays balanced, the base is right [00:10:11] [01:04:55]. Uneven spacing produces "extreme kind of stretching" on the smoothed result [00:52:13]. Measure: `MM.balance` on the base grid, before support loops (a support loop is an intentional thin band and always exceeds 2).

**Density comes from the narrowest feature.** "The shortest distance between the two edges" sets the minimum density; balance the rest to it [00:16:47] [00:17:19]. Stop at the density you will use: extra topology is waste unless you will cut details into it [00:53:51] [00:54:24].

**Loops prove quads are solved.** "If you notice that your loop is breaking somewhere, that means you still have an open area where quad is not solved" [00:18:57]. A loop that "suddenly stops" can be a double edge from an empty extrude, visible only in smooth preview [00:39:22] [00:39:53].

**Triangle protocol.** "When in doubt, remove the triangle and then bring them back once you have more or less everything solved" [00:20:36]. A triangle is only a problem if it breaks a loop you need or, on a curved surface, bends the curvature [00:21:45] [00:29:59]. Paying "the price of a triangle" for better distribution is fine [00:30:32]. Judge with a metallic material on the smooth preview: if the surface holds, the triangle stays [00:30:32] [00:31:05] (frame 00:58:24: Standard Surface, metalness 1, roughness about 0.5, value uncertain in the note). After one Catmull-Clark smooth, triangles become quads [01:22:33]. Agent tool: `MM.cage_report` sorts triangles into flat (may stay if the highlight holds) and curved (remove, solve, reinstate only if it holds) [added threshold: every face touching the triangle within 1 degree of its plane].

**Offset before cutting; support loops last on the base.** Before cutting a part out, extrude the faces with an Offset and no translation (frame 00:39:05: Thickness 0, Local Translate Z 0, Offset > 0, Divisions 1, Keep Faces Together on): the offset ring is the support loop, "the job is already kind of done for us" [00:38:16] [00:38:49]. Support loops "emphasize the highlights of the holding corners" and go around every edge that must read sharp, after the base is balanced [00:27:46] [00:28:21]. Inner support loop of an opening: a small inward extrusion, or points connected following the outer edge [00:42:42].

**Corner routing template.** "If you're not sure, always take a cube, select every single edge, bevel": the beveled cube's corner is exactly how support loops connect at a three-edge corner [00:43:49] [00:44:24] [00:46:34].

**Density vs redirection.** Low density needs redirected edges and shows slight pinching; high density needs no redirection and does not pinch [01:09:20] [01:10:26]. The decision is per corner and per shot: write down the narrowest feature (the shortest distance between two edges that must both exist, such as a groove, a seam gap or a recess wall) as the minimum density [00:17:19], then let camera distance pick density or redirection [01:39:46]. Redirection on curvature is acceptable on background props, not on a hero [01:00:27] [01:01:32]. Compared side by side on a glossy material: medium density with a softer corner shows "almost nothing pinching"; redirected with a sharper corner, "a lighter pinching" [01:38:09] [01:38:41].

**Cylinders.** "We can add horizontal edges the way we want to, but the vertical edges we cannot" [00:57:43]; vertical cuts "destroyed the structure of the cylinder" [00:58:17]. Curvature is set by neighboring edges; to add topology and keep it, smooth and subdivide [00:58:49] [00:59:55] [01:16:27]. Sides in multiples of four (20, 24, 28, 32): model one quarter and duplicate; 26 rejected [01:11:30] [01:12:04] [01:12:36]. Vertical edges must line up with the cut; 28 was upgraded to 32 [01:15:55] [01:18:41]. Cap with quads, not a fan: remove the cap edges, big quads first (frames 01:12:18 fan, 01:15:41 quad cap) [01:13:42] [01:14:16]. On curved parts, create the support loop before the detail [01:19:49]. Agent tools: `MM.cylinder_sides_for`, `MM.build_quad_cylinder`.

**Soft corners.** Real corners have falloff; razor-sharp corners are rare and distance makes corners read sharp anyway [01:17:36] [01:18:09]. Polycount agrees for bake highs: round edges more than in real life so they read at game size (PC § Edge Thickness).

**Fixes he shows.** Loop takes an unexpected path, pinch streak: select the loop, reroute [00:12:54] [00:13:28]. Support loop spread too thick through a triangle: reposition, leave the harmless triangle in the middle [01:27:01] [01:28:08]. Faceted patch after mirror or bridge: these are hard edges, soften them [01:28:38] [01:29:14]; on a cage, every edge is soft before the high is smoothed (`MM.cage_report` hard edges 0). Outside support loop missing because the inside one was not created first on a curved part: rebuild it with an absolute-distance loop offset [01:29:14] [01:30:56]. Bridge wide or twisted: adjust twist or bridge in two parts [01:34:21].

**Limits.** He never discusses creases, weighted normals or mid-poly game bevels; those come from the docs and Polycount below.

## Jessica Dru Johnson: performance topology (`ZiYEO49B768`, CG Futures 2018)

Senior Cinematic Artist, Blizzard in-game cinematics (StarCraft 2, World of Warcraft, Diablo 3, Overwatch). Signal between [01:10:50] and [01:34:12].

- **Topology is for the people downstream:** "if you're making production art you have to make it work for everybody around you" [01:10:50]; ask riggers and animators; "a Disney style of doing things and a Pixar style... both are completely valid" [01:11:57].
- **Isolation loops** bound controls: one loop around a sub-part sets where one control's falloff stops [01:13:39] [01:14:11]. Face isolation set: full face, muzzle, mouth, nose, eyes, ears, brows (frame 01:26:29) [01:26:20] [01:26:54].
- **Concentric, parallel loops;** a loop spiraling down a limb is "bananas", artifacts and muddy weights; concentric loops let a rigger say "this joint affects exactly to here and no further" [01:12:33] [01:21:55].
- **Rule of threes:** "one edge loop for control, two edge loops for support" [01:15:51]. Kneecap, elbow, knuckles isolated with one inset or extrude [01:14:44] [01:16:27] [01:17:01]; knuckles three loops each [01:17:34]; thumb needs a loop around plus the palm-line isolation or it "collapses and shears" [01:18:38] [01:20:49]. Wrist: one transition loop, no fancy topology [01:21:23].
- **Shear:** a quad should get longer or shorter along the motion; shearing flickers between triangulations or collapses; kill jaw shearing with one loop from lip corner straight back to the jaw corner, scaled flat in Z [01:13:06] [01:30:10].
- **Mouth and eyes:** double circle at the lip edge, lengthwise loops meeting at one center point, mouth modeled flat [01:28:31] [01:29:03]; lid loops meet when closed (frames 01:31:17, 01:31:23).
- **Deliver a neutral face** (flat lids, flat mouth) even if it "looks super peaceful" [01:00:30].
- **Engine limits are topology limits:** 4 bone influences per vertex; one extra mouth-corner span needed 5 [00:44:17] [00:59:24]. Standardized base topology took face skinning from three days to 30 minutes [00:58:51].
- **Shoulder:** her animators prefer tube topology over deltoid isolation; isolate the scapula, and stop its loop at the elbow (frame 01:24:05) [01:24:07] [01:24:41].
- **Triangles:** keep quads, hide unavoidable triangles where there is little animation [01:12:33].
- **Scope by performance:** emotional, action, background; a background character may get textures and no modeling (frame 01:33:55) [01:33:28].
- **Deciding condition vs Mario:** she minimizes spans for bone budgets, he adds density for highlights. Static offline hero: density buys quality. Real-time deforming asset: every span costs influences and weights.

## Antony Ward (antCGi): organic base mesh and deformation test (2021 to 2022)

Game-industry veteran, author of _Game Character Development with Maya_ (book verified, studio list unverified). Four lessons of #ModelingInMaya; captions machine-translated ("ribs" = edges, "cook" = weld).

**Blocking (`-mWWeFv07SI`).** Autosave on: 10 backups, every 10 minutes, one fixed folder [00:02:01] [00:03:05]. Low-division primitives: torso cylinder 12 x 8, arm and leg 8 x 6, chest sphere 6 x 6 [00:05:28] [00:11:23]. Delete caps, delete history per object (never Delete All by Type in a file with deformers), then freeze [00:13:19] [00:13:50]. Insert with Edge Flow on curved areas, or new edges flatten the form [00:16:04]. Target Weld, not V-snap: a snapped vertex is still two vertices [00:17:07]. Delete Edge, not the Delete key, so vertices go too [00:19:16]. When Fill Hole fails: extrude an edge, collapse it to a triangle, target weld [00:19:48] [00:20:51]. Match loop counts before joining limbs [00:27:43]. Loops follow fold lines (hip crease) [00:30:21]. "The fewer edge loops a model has, the better it will look and perform" [00:18:44].

**Mirror and symmetry (`QW8w15J00Ok`).** Mesh > Mirror: X, direction minus, Merge Border, threshold Custom 0.001; Automatic ate the center topology [00:01:52] [00:02:24] [00:02:56]. After every mirror, hunt stray center vertices; "select all vertices and Delete" removes winged ones [00:03:29] [00:04:40]. Red symmetry components mean no match: edits there are one-sided; "I don't fully trust the symmetry tool" [00:06:09] [00:13:44]. A pole above the chest "will cause problems with model deformation... a kink": bevel the loop open and reroute [00:09:58] [00:10:29]. Shoulder loop wraps the shoulder and passes under the chest (deltoid, pectoralis) [00:11:03]. A Relax error is a topology error: Cleanup non-manifold in fix mode, then check for split vertices [00:13:13] [00:13:56]. Finger extrusion rings are joint positions [00:19:24]. Agent tool: `MM.mirror_merge` (seam-only merge, near-miss snap, verified counts).

**Face (`RlNnp4qQIrU`).** Openings start as one quad extruded with its middle deleted [00:01:46]. Loops follow facial muscles [00:02:51]. Upper and lower lids and lips carry the same loop count [00:05:22] [00:12:00]. Mouth-corner loop "needs to run back under her cheek", and the loop from the mouth corner around the head is "a key element for when we come to generate blend shapes" [00:09:32] [00:23:15]. Model lids over the real eyeballs [00:08:24]. Judge unsmoothed too [00:19:11]. Bevel if exported, crease if not: creases are lost on export [00:28:12] [00:35:01]. Placeholder ears, brows and lashes early, or the head "looks too much like an alien" [00:29:50].

**Deformation test (`x07USYlvu2o`).** Test before texturing [00:00:00]. Throwaway chain with a root anchor, bind with Weight Distribution Neighbors, pose, flood by region, smooth [00:03:34] [00:04:12] [00:06:57]. Separate faults: at the elbow "the topology is okay so it would need something extra in the rig"; at wrist and ankle the flow must run across the joint, a modeling fix [00:10:27] [00:11:04] [00:17:37]. Proof: the updated elbow topology deforms better with the same weights [00:16:24]. Twist is judged mid-limb [00:08:40]. Delete Non-Deformer History, never History, on a skinned mesh [00:12:41]. Unbind with Delete history to return to bind [00:13:49]. Face tests with joints, because blend shapes break on topology change [00:19:00]. Walk, jog, run, then a ROM file [00:19:43] [00:21:10]. Agent tool: `MM.bend_test` (same chain and bind settings for every topology variant, rest shape checked after teardown).

**Model evaluation before rigging (`e74KphYwMww`, rigging batch).** What the rigger checks on arrival: working units cm, size to spec, feet on the ground, centered on X, frozen main group, eyes looking dead ahead, loops around knuckles and knees, mouth bag for a face rig, A pose, symmetric parts combined so weights can be mirrored [00:02:52] to [00:25:47]. "Game model optimization never justifies sacrificing deformation" [00:22:50].

## Documentation

**Maya 2027 Help, polygon tools (POLY).** Smart Bevel (2027) follows shape not topology, is recommended after booleans and on messy geometry; Width is in world units; Depth is a profile (1.0 flat chamfer, 0 to 1 rounded, negative concave with 2+ segments); Filter defaults to Selected Edges (also Hard Edges, Edges by Angle); segments 1 hard, 2 to 3 for most hard surface; "use the lowest segment count that achieves the desired visual result"; 2027.1 fixed spikes, sawtooth corners and arc welding, 2027.2 is up to 20x faster. Legacy bevel: Fractional (default) caps width at the shortest edge and cannot invert, Absolute can; defaults 1 segment, depth 1, chamfer on, smoothing angle 30. Crease value N creases N subdivision levels; the maximum visible crease equals the mesh's subdivision level; Crease Tool and Crease Sets "cannot be used interchangeably on the same components". Smooth Mesh Preview is display only for Maya's rendering. Edit Edge Flow: at most 2 non-adjacent loops. Bridge: same mesh, equal border counts, consistent normals; a bow tie means inconsistent normals, a spiral an offset. Booleans: manifold inputs, uniform normals, meshes selected, not their group. Topological symmetry: equal halves, a center edge, never edit the seam edge.

**Arnold subdivision settings (SS doc).** "Arnold will also render this preview smoothed state in the final image": preview plus Arnold iterations multiply (2 preview levels + 4 Arnold iterations on 426,936 polygons is 1.7 billion). This contradicts the Maya help's "display only" for Arnold renders: set subdivision in one place (`MM.subdiv_setup`).

**Maya 2027 Help, fundamentals (FUND).** A mesh is a transform plus a shape. Deleting history bakes the object and can change its appearance. Parenting geometry with history can transform it twice; `group` handles it, `parent` does not. Freeze and Reset differ. Optimize Scene Size has no undo. Reduce is decimation, not retopology. macOS: Ctrl is Control, Alt is Option.

**Pixar OpenSubdiv modeling tips (OSD).** Regular faces, few extraordinary vertices, manifold. About 6 spans for accurate circles, 4 for background. High valence (above 4) causes waviness and cost; GPU limit about 27; cap revolution shapes with valence-4 quads. Pentagons "sparingly" at saddles. Crease sharpness 0 to 10, above 5 rarely needed, cost grows with sharpness; crease sets named with the value (`topDeck_2`); infinite creases tear under displacement.

**Polycount wiki (PC).** Poly count in games means triangles; vertex count is the real cost because UV seams, hard edges and material changes duplicate vertices; a hard edge on a UV seam is free. Hard edges on UV seams and on mechanical bends above about 45 degrees. Bevels improve silhouette and highlights but create thin triangles and more vertices; if you bevel, edit the normals so big flats stay flat. Triangulate before baking and export the same triangulation; mirror the triangulation too; check the triangulation in the engine and triangulate by hand a quad that became a ridge instead of a valley (§ Polygons Vs. Triangles, workflow step 7; agent tool `MM.triangulated_copy(toward=high)`). Mismatched tangent bases between baker and renderer show as artifacts (§ Smoothing Groups & Hard Edges). Bake the whole mirrored model with mirrored UVs offset by 1 unit. Floating geometry works for normal maps; for AO make floaters separate with shadow casting off. Keep the low contiguous, one connected chunk where possible, to avoid overlap bake errors, aliased edges and UV waste (§ Contiguous Meshes). LODs can introduce normal-map seams (§ Level of Detail Models); straight UV shell borders help LOD generation (§ UV Tutorials & Threads). Poles in flat areas, away from deformation.

**Epic, FBX Static Mesh Pipeline (FBXUE).** The export origin is the pivot, often at a corner for grid snapping; triangulate in the DCC; FBX 2020.2; UCX_ collision named after the render mesh; Smoothing Groups on. The Maya scene stays Y up; the FBX exporter's axis conversion (or the importer) handles a Z-up engine (pipeline FBX note).

**Maya 2027 Help, UV Toolkit (UVDOC), for the handoff boundary only.** Unfold3D refuses non-manifold meshes and Maya sends Unfold a triangulated mesh, so non-planar faces can hide non-manifold cases: clean before unfolding (§ Prepare a UV mesh, step 2). Room space stays at the 2 px default (§ Room Space Options). Unfold3D pins unselected UVs automatically (§ Unfold a UV mesh). Optimized game maps may lower density on unseen faces and share sheets (§ Optimizations). The UV work itself is scenario-maya-retopology-uv's; this skill guarantees the mesh-side preconditions (procedures P17).

## Where the experts disagree, and the deciding condition

| Question                       | Positions                                                                                                                                                                          | Decide by                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Triangles                      | Mario keeps them on flats, removes on curves; Jessica hides them away from animation; antCGi removes all on a sculpt base; Polycount: fine on game lows if every edge has a reason | static flat vs curved vs deforming; sculpt base vs final asset vs game low                                   |
| Density vs redirection         | Mario: density on heroes; Jessica: minimal spans for bone budgets                                                                                                                  | offline static hero vs real-time deforming                                                                   |
| Sharp edges                    | support loops (Mario), creases (OSD: cheaper), chamfer plus normals or hard edges on UV seams (Polycount), bevel for export (antCGi)                                               | does the surface stay in a subdivision renderer or leave for an engine                                       |
| Legacy vs Smart Bevel          | legacy predictable on clean quads and matches tutorials; Smart after booleans and messy meshes                                                                                     | clean hand-built quads vs boolean or dense input, and the point release                                      |
| Clean-scene rebuild            | FlippedNormals: OBJ round trip                                                                                                                                                     | OBJ for a single-UV-set sculpt base; MA carrier otherwise [added]                                            |
| Pivot                          | FlippedNormals: world or object center, consistently; Epic: origin, often a corner                                                                                                 | film vs engine placement                                                                                     |
| Shoulder topology              | Jessica's animators: tube; antCGi: deltoid and pectoral loop                                                                                                                       | rig shapes via correctives vs via topology: ask the rigger                                                   |
| Mirror tool vs symmetry        | antCGi: symmetry for shaping, mirror for topology truth                                                                                                                            | agents: model half and `MM.mirror_merge` [added]                                                             |
