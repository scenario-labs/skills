# Critique rubric: judging a model like the experts

Use at every stage gate, on numbers first (toolkit output), then on the sheets you opened. Each line: what to look at, pass, fail, and what to do. Sources in brackets; [added] is this skill's own threshold.

## 1. Purpose and budget

| Look at             | Pass                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Fail and fix                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Written brief       | destination, budget or "not given", camera distance, deformation, size, texture set all written down                                                                                                                                                                                               | missing: stop and write the assumption; never model "in general" (Mario [00:00:32]; On Mars [00:04:55])                                                                              |
| Triangles vs budget | inside the lead's band; if none, reported with the reason for each kept edge and anchored on On Mars' bands: hero seen large on screen 20k to 30k, secondary or tertiary props at 10 to 15% of the screen "much less", background 10k or 5k (grenade-class prop, [00:03:15] [00:04:21] [00:04:55]) | over: strip flat edges, merge on flats, triangulate to terminate loops (On Mars [00:09:22] [00:26:34]); never describe the grenade figure as "the only number" or invent its context |
| Density decision    | the narrowest feature and its width written down, grid spacing there equal to it; per corner, density (hero, close camera: no pinch) or redirection (background: fewer polygons, slight pinch) chosen and stated                                                                                   | density picked by habit: re-derive from the narrowest feature (Mario [00:17:19]) and the camera distance ([01:09:20] [01:10:26] [01:39:46])                                          |
| Engine vertices     | `split_report` engine_verts_est and split_ratio reported; hard edges sit on UV seams                                                                                                                                                                                                               | hard edges off seams on a normal-mapped low inflate vertices for nothing (Polycount)                                                                                                 |
| Density purpose     | every loop serves silhouette, a highlight, a bake curvature, a UV seam or a joint                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | "not really contributing to anything": remove (Mario [00:54:24]; On Mars [00:06:54])                                                                                                 |

## 2. Silhouette and forms (sheet: silhouette, clay)

| Look at                          | Pass                                                                                    | Fail and fix                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Silhouette per view vs reference | proportions and gesture read in black on white                                          | wrong proportion: fix at blockout, not with detail                                                |
| Real size                        | bounding box within 2% of spec [added]; stands on the grid, centered where it should be | rescale before anything else (FlippedNormals [00:23:28]; antCGi e74K [00:02:52])                  |
| Facing                           | front view shows the front, upright, not mirrored                                       | rotate and freeze (FlippedNormals [00:05:07])                                                     |
| High vs low silhouettes          | alphas overlap at game distance                                                         | a removed silhouette edge: put it back (On Mars [00:07:27])                                       |
| Corners at shot distance         | slightly soft, falloff visible                                                          | razor corners read sharp anyway at distance, and bake badly (Mario [01:17:36]; PC Edge Thickness) |

## 3. Topology (sheet: wire; numbers: mx_audit, MM.balance)

| Look at                       | Pass                                                                                                                                                            | Fail and fix                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| N-gons on delivery            | 0                                                                                                                                                               | fix by hand; never let Cleanup triangulate them (FlippedNormals [00:11:50])                                                                                                                         |
| Triangles                     | on flat static areas, or terminating loops on a game low                                                                                                        | on a curved sub-D surface they kink the highlight; on a deforming area they pinch: remove, solve the rest, reinstate only if the metal test holds (Mario [00:20:36] [00:29:59]; Jessica [01:12:33]) |
| Base grid balance             | `edges_over_limit` 0 on the base grid (support loops excluded)                                                                                                  | "not double" (Mario [00:08:31]): slide or add loops, or reroute                                                                                                                                     |
| Loop continuity               | loops run all the way across, closed where they should close                                                                                                    | a loop that breaks = an unsolved area; a loop that stops dead = a hidden double extrude (Mario [00:18:57] [00:39:22])                                                                               |
| Coincident points             | `coincident_vertices` 0 pairs (except intended stacked parts)                                                                                                   | Target Weld equivalent: move then `polyMergeVertex` (antCGi [00:17:07])                                                                                                                             |
| Poles                         | valence 3 and 5 on flat, non-deforming areas; nothing above 5 on a hard-surface cap; nothing near 27                                                            | fan caps: quad cap (Mario [01:14:16]; OSD); pole on the chest: reroute (antCGi [00:09:58])                                                                                                          |
| Cylinders                     | side count a multiple of 4, vertical edges aligned with the cut, only ring loops added                                                                          | vertical cuts wobble the highlight: rebuild with more sides or smooth once (Mario [00:57:43] [01:18:41])                                                                                            |
| Round features on a cage      | about 6 spans (hero), 4 (background)                                                                                                                            | fewer: under-resolved; many more: waste (OSD)                                                                                                                                                       |
| Cage review before smoothing  | `MM.cage_report` `ready_to_smooth`: hard edges 0, no fan caps, n-gons 0; every curved triangle removed or proven by the highlight                               | faceted patch = hard edge: soften (Mario [01:28:38]); fan: quad cap; curved triangle: remove, solve, reinstate only if the metal test holds ([00:20:36])                                            |
| Support loops on curved parts | cut before the detail they hold                                                                                                                                 | built around the detail afterwards: edges move, the outside loop goes missing; rebuild (Mario [01:19:49] [01:29:14])                                                                                |
| Game low: separate shells     | each shell justified (`polyEvaluate(shell=True)`); parts touching the body welded in, or their hidden faces deleted                                             | intersecting pieces: overlap bake errors, aliased edges, UV space on hidden faces (PC § Contiguous Meshes)                                                                                          |
| Game low: triangulation       | exported triangulated; on non-planar quads the diagonal follows the high (`triangulated_copy(toward=high)`, `diagonals_wrong_after` 0); engine import looked at | a ridge where the high has a valley: flip the diagonal (PC § Polygons Vs. Triangles)                                                                                                                |
| Game low: round corners       | radius edges more than 2 to 3, evenly spaced, one on the corner's highest point                                                                                 | too few: bake artifacts on the curvature (On Mars [00:25:29] [00:26:34])                                                                                                                            |
| Manifold                      | 0 non-manifold, lamina, zero-length edges, zero-area faces                                                                                                      | Cleanup is fine for these (FlippedNormals [00:11:15]); re-audit after                                                                                                                               |

## 4. Surface quality (highlight review, closeups)

| Look at                                      | Pass                                                                                                                                                                       | Fail and fix                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stripes on metal across every cut and corner | continuous, bending smoothly                                                                                                                                               | dark streak or kink next to a pole, triangle or tight loop: pinch; reroute or add density (Mario frames 00:12:45, 01:38:42)                 |
| Big flat faces                               | stripes straight, no gradient                                                                                                                                              | gradient on a flat: non-planar face, or normals not weighted on a beveled low (PC Edited Vertex Normals)                                    |
| Curved surfaces                              | even stripe spacing                                                                                                                                                        | stretching = uneven loop spacing (Mario [00:52:13])                                                                                         |
| Faceting on a sub-D cage                     | none; `cage_report` hard edges 0                                                                                                                                           | hard edges left after mirror, bridge or boolean: soften (Mario [01:28:38])                                                                  |
| Flipped faces                                | `mx_audit` inconsistent winding 0 (single faces) and `MM.inverted_shells` 0 (whole closed shells); in a GUI, nothing black with two-sided lighting off or backface culling | two-sided lighting on hides both (FlippedNormals [00:20:47]): reverse or conform, check bridges, booleans and negative-scale freezes (POLY) |
| Normals sheet (unlit)                        | one color field per surface direction, no inverted patch                                                                                                                   | colors show direction, not backfaces: it complements, never replaces, the checks above                                                      |
| Crease look at render level                  | sharpness as intended at the final subdivision                                                                                                                             | value above the render level does nothing more; value 10 tears under displacement (POLY; OSD)                                               |

## 5. Deformation (characters, creatures, anything skinned)

| Look at                 | Pass                                                                                                                          | Fail and fix                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Loops across each joint | 1 control + 2 support (Jessica [01:15:51]); loops run across wrist and around ankle                                           | loops along the limb: rebuild (antCGi [00:11:04] [00:17:37])                                                                               |
| Isolation loops         | closed around eyes, mouth, full face, knuckles, thumb                                                                         | open: controls bleed into each other (Jessica [01:14:11])                                                                                  |
| Mouth and lids          | same loop count upper and lower; mouth-corner loop runs under the cheek and round the head; lids built over the real eyeballs | blend shapes and blinks fail (antCGi RlNnp4qQIrU [00:05:22] [00:09:32] [00:08:24])                                                         |
| Neutral pose            | flat lids, flat mouth, eyes looking dead ahead, A or T pose as agreed                                                         | posed face or eyes: animation and eye aims break (Jessica [01:00:30]; antCGi e74K [00:08:14])                                              |
| `bend_test`             | no normal flips; min area ratio not worse than the previous topology under the same settings                                  | collapse at a clean ring: flag a corrective for scenario-maya-deformation; collapse from flow: fix topology (antCGi [00:10:27] [00:16:24]) |
| Influence budget        | loop layout that keeps vertices within the engine's 4 influences where the target demands it                                  | one extra span can need a fifth bone (Jessica [00:59:24])                                                                                  |
| Normals                 | not locked                                                                                                                    | locked normals do not follow skinning: unlock before rigging                                                                               |

## 6. Scene hygiene (mx_validate profile "model", handoff_report)

| Look at                                 | Pass                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Fail and fix                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| History, transforms                     | no history, frozen meshes and groups (eye groups excepted)                                                                                                                                           | `validate(fix=SAFE_FIXES)`; freeze; never Delete History on a skinned mesh (antCGi [00:12:41])                              |
| Names                                   | unique short names, `_geo` (film) or engine names (`SM_`, `UCX_`), sides from the character's point of view, no defaults (`pCube1`, `polySurface92`, `group3`); stated and checked, not only applied | rename at creation; `mx_audit` default_name, duplicate_short_name; duplicates are always a fail (FlippedNormals [00:18:16]) |
| Cleanup                                 | run with only lamina, non-manifold, zero-length (and zero-area) switches: `MM.cleanup_technical` `ok`                                                                                                | tessellation options on: n-gons triangulated at random; undo, fix by hand (FlippedNormals [00:10:44] [00:11:50])            |
| Render stats                            | defaults                                                                                                                                                                                             | one unchecked box cost two days (FlippedNormals [00:02:35])                                                                 |
| Smooth preview                          | off at every save, render subdivision in the Arnold attributes                                                                                                                                       | files heavier, and Arnold renders the preview state on top of its own iterations (FlippedNormals [00:24:00]; Arnold doc)    |
| Subdivision                             | set in one place                                                                                                                                                                                     | preview plus Arnold iterations multiply the polygon count                                                                   |
| Layers, sets, namespaces, unknown nodes | none except crease sets and shading                                                                                                                                                                  | delete, or rebuild the scene (FlippedNormals [00:08:31])                                                                    |
| Version                                 | new saved version; source untouched                                                                                                                                                                  | never overwrite the published file (FlippedNormals [00:25:06])                                                              |

## 7. Tool traps (kept here, out of SKILL.md's table)

| Trap                                                               | Looks like                                  | Fix                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Absolute legacy bevel on small parts                               | inside-out faces                            | Fractional, or Smart Bevel after freezing (POLY)                                          |
| Smart Bevel assumed to follow the legacy 2-segment box-corner rule | n-gons at corners, or a claim nobody tested | the rule is from the legacy bevel (FlippedNormals [00:18:18]); read `MM.bevel` `problems` |
| Automatic mirror threshold                                         | center topology eaten                       | `MM.mirror_merge` (Border, 0.001, antCGi QW8w15J00Ok [00:02:56])                          |
| Boolean on a group or non-manifold input                           | garbage result                              | select meshes, conform normals (POLY)                                                     |
| Parenting geometry with history                                    | object moves twice                          | delete history first, or `group` (FUND)                                                   |
| Delete History on a skinned mesh                                   | skin gone                                   | Non-Deformer History (`bakePartialHistory`) (antCGi x07USYlvu2o [00:12:41])               |
| Component ids read on one mesh or topology, used on another        | wrong edges deleted, silhouette edges lost  | `MM.component_record` and `MM.carry_components` (P18)                                     |

## 8. The report

Honest reports list: what was measured (numbers), which sheets were opened and what they showed, what was assumed (budget, camera distance), what is pending for the receiver, and what was not verified (bake, engine import, a real rig). A model is not done before its highlight and review sheets have been looked at.
