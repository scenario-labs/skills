## Measured

- **One Scenario inpaint** on a 512 px owl photo, 2026-10-19: ellipse around the iris, alpha
  polarity, 2 outputs. Output size, whole-frame re-render, mask filling, seam numbers. The two takes
  are the fixture of the live suite's seam tests.
- **`test_generative_live.py`** (9 tests, Photoshop 27.11, macOS): capture in four polarities, check
  flags, placement through the mask file, a placement aimed inside a hidden markup group moved out
  of it, verify with `edited`, expand, tile plan.
