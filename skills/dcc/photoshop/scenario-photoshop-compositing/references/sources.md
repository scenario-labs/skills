# Sources behind scenario-photoshop-compositing

Videos were read through their captions (manual or auto-generated). Timestamps are caption chunk
starts. Titles that name a generative model, a person outside the video or emoji are paraphrased and
marked "(paraphrased)". Credentials come from the channel and the video itself; "self-described"
marks a credential only the presenter states. Videos from 2023 and 2024 stay because the technique
they show (Overlay noise, inverted High Pass, Curves dodge and burn, Select and Mask) has not
changed.

## Measured

- **`test_composite_live.py`** (10 tests, Photoshop 27.11, macOS): `fix_halo`, `shadows`,
  `match_tone`, `match_grain`, `blend_if`, `channel_pick`, `subject_mask` on a sample photo and a
  fringed disc; `match_grain` also on an element with other layers above it, the grain layer landing
  directly above the element and clipped to it; and on a second element, which got its own grain
  layer while the first element's grain pixels stayed unchanged, after a name clipped to the first
  element was refused.
- **`test_retouch_live.py`** (3 tests): frequency separation at 8 and 16 bits with `verify_fs`;
  `suggest_radius`, `highpass_smoother` and `dodge_burn`, each invisible until revealed.
- **`test_gates_live.py`**: the skin line on a flat skin patch, texture on noise against flat, halo
  on a clean and a fringed edge.
- **The lead's measured round trip:** a Scenario image-edit result re-rendered the whole frame
  (outside mean 17 to 18) until masked.
