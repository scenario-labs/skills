## Documentation

Adobe's help pages were not re-read for this skill: the help site refused automated reads on
2026-10-19. Menu paths come from the expert videos and Photoshop's own menus, and are marked not
tested.

- **Adobe 3D FAQ** (https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/kb/3d-faq.html): Photoshop's legacy 3D
  features were removed in July 2024. Seen through search results on 2026-10-18; the page itself did
  not load.

## Measured

- **`test_mockup_live.py`** (15 tests, Photoshop 27.11, macOS, 2026-10-19): `placeholder` without
  and with a group mask and with `size=`, `fit_quad` on four quads in a row, on a placed result, on
  a PNG with transparent margins, a clipped layer and an active selection, `fit_cylinder` on a
  checkerboard, `corners`, `quad_size`, `replace_design` in `exact` and `cover` (print proportions
  kept), `lighting`, `displace`, `batch`, `colorways` (clipped and masked, with and without the
  Curves match, on gray strips and on a white and a black shirt), and a fit refused on a smart
  object with Smart Filters. Fixtures: a 1000x800 document with 40 px gray strips, a white 1000x800
  document, and two 360x480 shirts with folds made with Pillow. Results in
  [expert-notes.md](expert-notes.md).
- **Colorway math offline**, 2026-10-19: the toolkit's own `garment_tones`, `tone_curve` and
  `swatch_check` plus Photoshop's Color blend, on a white tee and a black sweatshirt cut from frames
  of Anders [rrxTM5AZALU]. No Photoshop and no product photo shot for the test. Relative fold
  contrast, (p90 - p10) / p50: the white tee 0.21 before and after navy, red and sand, and white
  kept the blank (no curve); the black sweatshirt 1.13 before, then 0.57 (navy) and 0.39 (red), not
  flagged, 0.14 (sand) and 0.004 (white), flagged `flat`. Delta E 0.04 to 0.09 and folds 0.998 to
  1.0 for navy, red and sand on both.
- **One visual check** on a generated tote photo, 2026-10-19: a design flat, then lit, then
  displaced, judged on the whole frame and on a zoomed crop of the creases.
- **Platform checks on 27.11:** the custom warp descriptor, the scale-up rule for custom warps,
  `warpNone` against a flat reset, Convert to Smart Object margins, the `nonAffineTransform`
  self-check, Replace Contents scale and renaming, the Displace map re-read, History snapshots,
  clipping chains, Blend If read-back, the Color blend constant. Details in
  [expert-notes.md](expert-notes.md).
