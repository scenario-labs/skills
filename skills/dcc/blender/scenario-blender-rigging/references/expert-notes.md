## 1. Placement, roll, hierarchy

- **Model check first.** Centered at the origin, feet on the floor, facing -Y, about 1.8 m, transforms applied: "A huge number of rigging problems people ask me about are simply due to skipping the model check" (CGDive A147p_zDwO4 [00:03:17]). Two kinds of symmetry: general and true (vertex for vertex); Snap to Symmetry only on parts meant to be symmetric, never on an intentionally asymmetric body (Q21AZnAyOJA [00:07:03] to [00:08:03]). The armature is created at the origin and never moved in Object Mode while rigging, or Symmetrize breaks (FzQVpzXcFX0 [00:31:47]).
- **Bones stand for bone, muscle and tissue.** Place them in the center of the volume they move; anatomy is a starting point (FzQVpzXcFX0 [00:05:34]). Spine pulled toward the middle of the body in side view instead of the S-curve (q-PRGvE1ico [00:05:02]); spine lengths evened out afterwards (A147p_zDwO4 [00:46:59]). Clavicle bone high and deep, buried between clavicle, traps and scapula (FhF54DZY34U [00:52:56], q-PRGvE1ico [00:07:29]); upper-arm head deep in the socket below the clavicle (q-PRGvE1ico [00:08:21]); head pivot "just below the ear" (q-PRGvE1ico [00:06:46]); finger bases at the knuckle line, deeper than the palm folds (QnCo0hzXKeQ [00:00:00]).
- **The bend is the IK specification.** Knees slightly forward, elbows slightly back, straight in the other view (CGDive FhF54DZY34U [00:50:42]); Demeter exaggerated Sintel's elbow bend until Rigify's pole landed behind, because roll changes did not fix the bend direction (f6im_0QVeuo [00:11:17], [00:11:49]). "In rigging, it is easier to bend something than to unbend it": straighten a drooping tail or close a mouth in the model before rigging (FhF54DZY34U [02:01:06]).
- **Roll is an animation-curve decision.** One local axis per hinge so the animator uses one F-curve ("your animator is going to hate you", q-PRGvE1ico [00:13:35]); rotating a new bone 180 deg in Front vs Side view gives opposite axes, so always look at the axes (q-PRGvE1ico [00:15:23]); fingers and thumb must curl on the same axis, roll the thumb 90 then 180 if it curls outward (QnCo0hzXKeQ [00:02:38]). Demeter's finger test: rotating each phalanx on local X must roll a clean fist with aligned knuckles, "regenerating the rig a million times" (f6im_0QVeuo [00:12:54]).
- **Hierarchy.** Every bone under one world-oriented root; audit by moving, rotating and scaling it (_6eyk1vembQ [00:22:01]). Disconnected parents are fine when connecting would move a pivot (QnCo0hzXKeQ [00:06:20]). Duplicated bones inherit their parent: clear parents and redo when confused (PdTOfJDdzgc [00:10:18]).
- [added, verified] Connected bones: setting a parent's tail moves the child's head; clear `use_connect` before re-parenting or the head jumps to the new parent's tail.

## 2. Rigify (CGDive, Demeter, Ivan Cappiello)

- **What Rigify is.** Building blocks, not one click: rig types assembled in a readable metarig that stores what to build and how pieces join (Ivan LLnXBvEnG7o [00:05:21]). The rig type sits on the first bone of a connected chain; names do not matter, connected parenting does (CGDive [02:41:21]); a chain continues until a branch, so the neck needs its own type or the head is swallowed by the spine (Demeter [00:09:07]). Name-based rigging is fragile ("never do that", Ivan [00:34:23]).
- **Choice of metarig.** Basic Human for a first rig (the full Human "is actually quite complex"), Human for fingers and face, Wolf for dogs, Basic Quadruped has no face or fingers (CGDive [00:48:27], [02:01:39], [02:05:31]). Pick leg types by anatomy: "you cannot rig a horse leg with the human arm" (Ivan [00:24:40]). Keep fitted metarigs in a library .blend and append them for similar characters; animation transfers because control names stay stable (Ivan [00:08:13], [01:13:24]).
- **Fitting rules.** Scale the metarig in Object Mode, apply scale; fit arms and fingers in Pose Mode and Apply Pose as Rest Pose, otherwise the generated rig differs in size or pose (CGDive [00:49:33], [01:14:47]). Move joints as units: "Always box select over their connection and move them like that. Never move them individually" (CGDive [01:16:24]). Face order: move the `face` parent in Y/Z only, center line first, surface bones with Snap to Face, lids aligned vertically, lip bones in the middle of the lip, jaw pivot "extremely important", eye bone centered on the eyeball's middle loop (CGDive [01:13:41] to [01:26:27]). Breast and pelvis bones only help automatic weights; delete them if you will paint the chest (CGDive [00:53:31]).
- **Face upgrade.** The Human metarig still ships the legacy monolithic face; Upgrade Face Rig after fitting, before generating; irreversible; extra lip bones only after it (CGDive [01:25:22], [02:21:50]; still true in 5.2.1 [verified]). Glue bones only work on exact overlaps with another control's head or tail (Ivan [00:50:25]).
- **Generation errors.** Almost all come from a joint that got separated: "bone position is disjoint" (neck or tail off the spine), "mouth corner counts differ" (lip ends apart), UI rows all 0, broken collection references, a limb generating nothing because delete-then-undo stripped its rig type (restore with Copy Type & Parameters) (CGDive [00:57:37] to [03:48:17]). "That should take care of about 90% of possible generation issues" (CGDive [02:23:42]).
- **Tuning lives on the metarig.** The generated rig is overwritten on regeneration: eyelids follow Z about 0.3, jaw Bottom Lip Influence 0.9, inner mouth chains spherical falloff off with falloff 0.4, B-Bone segments 1 on a distorting chin bone, Overwrite Widget Meshes on, eye bone size sets the eye target size and distance (CGDive [01:54:30] to [01:57:20]). Cosmetic tweaks with no parameter: a post-generation script (Demeter [00:31:30]). Roll the metarig bone to fix a widget's orientation (CGDive [03:57:18]).
- **Face weaknesses out of the box** (CGDive [01:52:46]): jaw opening without a nice expression until the pivot is right, hard to make a non-creepy smile, eye target too small and close, lower lids following the eye too much.
- **Organization.** Each component on its own collection, FK and tweak collections on the first bone of each component, a collection named `DEF` in the UI gives a deform-bones button for painting; for colors the top-most colored collection wins (CGDive [03:39:21], [03:55:07], [04:05:18]). Colors: Rigify's function colors, or left red / right blue / center yellow, "popular among professional animators"; ask the animator (CGDive [04:09:21], [04:13:46]). Demeter disables unified selection colors (f6im_0QVeuo [00:07:31]).
- **Studio iteration.** Generate early and often, toggle metarig and rig, pose constantly (Demeter [00:05:53], [00:11:49]). Cut what animators do not use: fist controls and IK fingers went (Demeter [00:14:33]). Hinge options that let the head stay upright while the torso rotates: "animators tend to ask for this" (Demeter [00:16:41]).
- **Pose-driven setups.** Rigify's action slots (ported from CloudRig): pose deform bones for "lips wide" in an action keyed at frames 0 and 10 with LINEAR interpolation, "you want it to be linear to what the animator is doing"; a Corrective slot with two triggers fixes combinations (Demeter [00:47:16], [00:52:14]).
- **Why segmented DEF bones.** Bendy bones are "a crazy mind-blowing feature" but do not export; segmented deform bones behave similarly and export (Ivan [01:04:42]).

## 3. Manual rig architecture (CGDive, Level 3)

- **Three classes.** DEF (deform), MCH (mechanism, never animated), controls (everything else; no CTRL prefix, Wayne [00:08:24]). Separate DEF completely once deform bones are final: duplicate deformers as DEF-, one Copy Transforms each to the original (now a "connective" bone, never moved afterwards), deform flags become trivial, weight painting shows only DEF bones, and the DEF hierarchy can be rebuilt for engines (twists 2 to 4 under twist 1, hand under the first forearm twist) (A147p_zDwO4 [03:24:48] to [03:46:27]).
- **Layered limbs.** A main MCH chain with twists follows an FK or an IK copy through two Copy Transforms named FK and IK, the IK influence driven by a 0/1 property; mechanisms can be added per layer without touching the others (A147p_zDwO4 [01:10:56], [01:17:56]). Automate on the shared MCH layer so elbow caps and correctives work in both modes (A147p_zDwO4 [05:59:00]).
- **Twist chains.** 1 twist not enough, 2 insufficient, 3 nice, 4 really nice, more than 5 invisible; Copy Rotation 1.0, 0.66, 0.33, about 0.1 from the driving end (thigh 0.05); forearm twists copy the hand Local/Local then Damped Track to it, because Damped Track never adds roll and so strips swing and keeps twist (A147p_zDwO4 [00:23:19], [00:29:33]; HhhSjGqetjo [00:07:56]). Upper-arm twists parented to the clavicle, Copy Location above Damped Track (Head/Tail 1), chained Copy Locations after disconnecting (connected parenting overrides Copy Location) (FzQVpzXcFX0 [00:50:11], Q21AZnAyOJA [00:35:38]).
- **Spine.** Keep deform bones in logical orientation; flip copies as hips and chest controls, Copy Rotation Local/Local Mix Add to curl, torso parents both, torso under root (A147p_zDwO4 [00:49:17] to [00:57:21]).
- **IK.** Target fully unparented from the chain (a disconnected-but-parented target is a dependency loop), chain 2, pole offset and parented to the target for legs (a foot twist twists the leg) or to the clavicle for arms (hand rotation should not swing the elbow); pole angle is right when toggling IK does not move the rest pose (-89.1 deg on his arm); IK ignores Limit Rotation and locks, so lock IK Y/Z on shins and forearms (QnCo0hzXKeQ [00:09:15]; A147p_zDwO4 [02:27:17], [02:28:27], [01:59:15]). Arm IK target = a copy of the hand (same orientation) and the IK hand copies its transforms (A147p_zDwO4 [02:25:06]). IK where limbs touch something, FK where they swing (A147p_zDwO4 [00:58:37]).
- **Foot.** Six pivots under one roll control; pivots meant to be used one at a time, enforced by Copy Rotation plus one-sided Limit Rotation; the ball stays manual; the toe stays flat through a Damped Track helper with the animatable toe parented under it ("constraint plus child"); the roll control parented to the ankle control, never under a pivot it drives (A147p_zDwO4 [01:47:24] to [01:57:35]; PdTOfJDdzgc [00:13:10]).
- **Limb isolation.** Never Inherit Rotation off (it ignores the whole hierarchy including root); use a parent MCH (child of pelvis/clavicle) and a follow MCH (no parent) with World Copy Location/Rotation/Scale, the property driving only Copy Rotation; Copy Scale keeps root scaling (A147p_zDwO4 [01:40:44], [02:07:08], [02:09:53]). Defaults he ships: legs IK, arms FK, follow 0 (A147p_zDwO4 [02:36:25]).
- **Hands.** Finger control: rotate = rotate, scale = curl (Rigify convention); base phalanx Copy Rotation Mix Add (parenting would scale the finger); middle and distal phalanges Transformation constraint Scale Z 0..1 to Rotation Z 90..0 with Extrapolate, because a driver would lock manual rotation (_6eyk1vembQ [00:08:07] to [00:12:42]). Palm bones 0.5 and 0.25 from both outer palms; "5 deg is already kind of extreme", no limits (_6eyk1vembQ [00:17:58], [00:20:14]).
- **Custom properties** on a pose bone of a dedicated settings bone (visible in the N panel in Pose Mode), step 1, exactly 0 or 1 in production; renaming a property breaks every driver path (A147p_zDwO4 [01:24:04], [01:36:25], [04:58:05]).
- **Symmetry.** Build one side, Symmetrize; after renames delete the other side and resymmetrize the whole limb; Symmetrize does not copy drivers; pose-mode X mirror is name-based, edit-mode X mirror enforces geometry (ErR4RysRy0g [00:41:19]; _6eyk1vembQ [00:44:39]). Delete plus a single undo can bring bones back without constraints and properties: undo further and verify, undo steps 256 (ErR4RysRy0g [00:39:18]; untested in 5.2).
- **Widgets.** Wire meshes in a hidden collection, local Y = bone length, wire width 2, shape where the animator expects to grab (foot rectangle at the sole), Override Transform to draw chest and hips shapes at the deforming bones, move the bone (pole distance, settings location) rather than offsetting the shape (aj_ACAE7K8I; doRG4Sko7Kg [00:45:12], [00:49:11], [00:51:00]).

## 4. Weighting

- **Recipe.** Blend Add, weight 1, strength about 0.2 (0.1 to 0.15 for blending), Auto Normalize always ("probably the most important option"), mirror only on truly symmetric meshes, Front Faces Only off, Zero Weights Active; click add, Ctrl subtract, Shift blur; "weight painting is just a lot of clicking" (CGDive tNW8uCs77MQ [00:02:06], 6zGVAStZP50 [00:14:40]; Demeter uses only Add, Subtract, Blur [00:23:46]).
- **Judge in pose.** Demeter paints with the character posed (f6im_0QVeuo [00:24:51]); CGDive scrubs a test animation (stride, full split, back kick, crouch, arm down/forward/up with the shoulder raised about 20 to 30 deg) and re-checks earlier poses after every change (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:13:25], [00:23:28], [00:53:23]); Dikko keys a test animation (ljW9YJeA79E [00:42:16]).
- **Where auto weights fail.** Waist (Demeter [00:20:33]), torso under the arm and chin (CGDive Ldki5txSn5U [00:28:13]), armpit/chest, crotch "red pants", heel, collar (6zGVAStZP50). Chest compression came from arm twists leaking onto the torso (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:28:02]). The upper arm has no business on the lower torso (CGDive FhF54DZY34U [01:34:30]).
- **Principles.** Hard over soft: "having your weights a little bit harder, which creates intersection between the geometry, is better than having too smooth weights" (FzQVpzXcFX0 [01:43:01]). Limit each area to 2 or 3 bones: "multiple bones are fighting for influence" (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:20:27]). Lock finished groups, mask the work area (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:16:21]). Exact seams between overlapping meshes with a Mix brush at 0.5, strength 1 (0.75 neck / 0.25 chest also good) (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:34:02], [00:46:47]). Join meshes to paint across them; weights survive join and separate (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:43:11]).
- **Cleanup habits.** Near-zero weights of far bones make blur explode: Clean Vertex Groups, then blur with the deform-bones subset (the default smooths only the active group, "pretty much always not what you want") (Demeter [00:27:03], [00:39:10]). Smooth does nothing where a bone has zero influence: add a little first (CGDive FhF54DZY34U [02:36:28]). Levels with a negative offset hardens, then Normalize All with Lock Active off (BajO2vgto80 [00:28:23]).
- **Silent failure.** "Bone Heat Weighting: failed to find solution for one or more bones" appears only in the status bar and the mesh does not move (CGDive FhF54DZY34U [01:29:49], BajO2vgto80 [00:00:33]). Fixes: scale 100x and re-weight (CGDive [02:24:16], partial on a bad mesh [verified]); a low-poly cage with a span at every joint, weighted and transferred (Dikko [00:23:01]); rigid parts assigned manually (BajO2vgto80 [00:01:24]); Voxel Heat Diffuse add-on (paid, merges close appendages; Dikko [00:45:32]). [added, verified] Voxel-remeshed proxy + Data Transfer, coarser voxels if the source has holes.
- **Clothes and accessories.** Auto weights on separate pieces fail by proximity (bands slide, rings slide, hands pull the shirt); transfer from the same cage or body to every piece, apply the Data Transfer, smooth all pieces with the same settings (Dikko [00:35:34] to [00:43:54]; CGDive whiskers FhF54DZY34U [02:30:39]). Detail meshes riding the body: Surface Deform (Demeter [00:02:09]).
- **Rigid parts.** Eyes, teeth, lashes, hard-surface islands: Remove from All Groups, assign 1.0 to one bone (CGDive FhF54DZY34U [01:42:31], 6zGVAStZP50 [00:18:02]); tongue on a shared mesh: Assign Automatic From Bones with only tongue vertices and bones selected (FhF54DZY34U [01:45:56]). With a Mirror modifier, a group named exactly `DEF-eye.R` is filled by vertex-group mirroring (Demeter [00:29:50]).
- **Deform flags** audited before (re)binding: root, IK targets and poles deforming pull the heel (tNW8uCs77MQ [00:05:37]). Undo automatic weights completely: clear parent, delete Armature modifiers, delete all groups (6zGVAStZP50 [00:01:50]). Parent With Empty Groups adds new deform bones without wiping painted weights (ykB4gBQgFOU [00:03:02]).
- **Modifier stack.** Armature above Subdivision (Demeter [00:20:00], Dikko [00:03:40]). Corrective Smooth: "unironically though I do recommend corrective smooth even for production", disabled while painting (Demeter [00:45:37]); Blender-only (CGDive SSL4mBAVaQc [00:04:06]).
- **Preserve Volume.** Fixes candy wrappers, bulges armpits and hips; confine it with a second Armature modifier without it, Multi Modifier on, painted `preserve` mask; the older inverted-group method distorts when the root moves (CGDive n_waiAcd8xY [00:00:48], [00:01:53]). Support bones improve auto weights for quick jobs but add groups to manage (rf3NDKXYpTk [00:02:50]).

## 5. Corrective shape keys

- Shape keys are linear offsets from the Basis and they add, which is what makes combinations work (82R2SjUxH2s [00:02:47], [00:03:21]). Make them after modeling, rigging and weights (82R2SjUxH2s [00:00:28]). A corrective only needs to look right in its pose: "Outside of it, it's nonsense" (7fHpWhM4rtc [00:03:05]).
- Drive from the bone active in both IK and FK (MCH main chain), shape the driver curve with a flat start so it kicks in late and reaches exactly 1 at the extreme, `-var` when roll makes the value negative, and drive ball joints from a swing-only bone (the first upper-arm twist) to avoid flips under twist (7fHpWhM4rtc [00:04:26], [00:08:40], [00:12:51], [00:18:02]).
- Sculpting in the posed state needs the Armature modifier shown in Edit Mode / on cage (7fHpWhM4rtc [00:02:29]); Pose Shape Keys (Demeter's add-on) duplicates the posed mesh, you sculpt, it writes the rest-space key and drivers (Rik _Ov0pFIOpug [00:15:27]). [added, verified] Headless equivalent: inverse skinning through the measured Jacobian, then smooth the displacement (the Smooth-brush pass) or the crease spikes.

## 6. Faces

- **Choose by purpose and style.** Rigify face for realistic or mildly stylized characters and many characters; hybrid bones + shapes for a stylized hero that must stay on model; action-constraint face for studios with a generator (digest synthesis of CGDive, Demeter, Rik). Shape keys are the industry standard for faces; Stop Staring set (about 16 shapes) for hand animation, 52 ARKit shapes for capture (CGDive gsS0qRztOPg [00:00:33], [00:01:08]). Jaw and eyes as bones (gsS0qRztOPg [00:01:40]).
- **Rik Schutte's production face (Storm).** Never one technique: joints, a lip ribbon guide (an extracted edge loop, "just weighted geometry"), pose-space correctives, lattices for squash and puffs (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:07:45], [00:11:24]). Three-curve "football" weight profile: sharp corner falloff, overlapping linear middles, clean shapes from weights alone (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:18:52]). Lip bones Stretch To the ribbon, eyelid bones Damped Track (constant length, they stay on the eyeball), guide bones pinned by Copy Location to one-vertex groups (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:21:43], [00:25:09], [00:46:37]). Brow and cheek shapes authored with weights on a temporary armature, only correctives sculpted, left side built then mirrored (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:30:48], [00:31:22]). Lock the depth axis on brow and mouth-corner controls and give depth its own control (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:34:11]). Widgets that ride the skin through Override Transform on a helper bone, which breaks the widget-drives-shape cycle; trade-off: F-curves then mix rotation and translation (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:39:32], [00:45:25]). Collections global / local / micro follow the animation workflow (_Ov0pFIOpug [00:47:11]).
- **Demeter.** There is no generic stylized face: "good luck ... I tried and I failed" (f6im_0QVeuo [00:34:46]). A single ring of lip bones cannot make a good smile: add an outer ring; lower lip fully to the jaw and upper lip to the head first, then lip bones with Multi-Paint; rotate the jaw open and paint so the mouth vertices visibly jump to the jaw; Sphere falloff when the view is inside the mesh (f6im_0QVeuo [00:36:25] to [00:43:27]).
- **CGDive bone face.** Lids follow the eye at about 0.15 (Copy Rotation Local, Mix Add), mouth corners follow the jaw at 0.5 through an MCH jaw copy, eye master + target with Damped Track, jaw master moved instead of rotated (ykB4gBQgFOU [00:06:55], [00:09:22]; A147p_zDwO4 [07:22:52]). Eye targets too close make the eyes react too fast (A147p_zDwO4 [07:29:45]); extend half-sphere eyes so extreme rotations do not show the edge (gsS0qRztOPg [00:11:21]).
- **CGDive shape-key face.** Wide follows the teeth horseshoe; smile sculpted on top of wide at 1; brows down as its own key (the skull), inner-brow-down seeded from inner-up at -1 via New Shape From Mix, which copies the whole MIX, so isolate the source first; test combinations (smile + jaw open) (gsS0qRztOPg [00:27:34] to [00:47:20]). L/R split with smoothed half-face groups on duplicated keys (gsS0qRztOPg [00:44:32]). On-face SK- bones flipped so the useful move is +Y, one bone drives several keys by axis and sign (`var` smile, `-var` frown, clamped by the key range), Limit Location with Affect Transform, right side width needs `-var` (gsS0qRztOPg [00:55:59] to [01:02:12]).
- **Rigify face** needs the parameter tuning in section 2; eyelids "are always problematic": isolate the upper lid, paint lightly on the outer lid bone to remove the zigzag (CGDive FhF54DZY34U [01:40:44]). Collar clipping into the neck: light positive weight on the first neck bone (FhF54DZY34U [01:49:53]).

## 7. Mechanical and special rigs

- Fundamentals are "no different": pivot, roll, weights; hinge joints XYZ Euler with Y/Z locked (FK) and IK locks (IK) (2XwZWJ_Hnac [00:00:32]; BajO2vgto80 [00:05:30], [00:14:20]). Split hard parts into islands and assign each to one bone; a slight pinch from an unsmoothed two-group seam reads as a hard part under soft material (BajO2vgto80 [00:01:24], [00:07:08]).
- Before automating, "ask yourself, is it worth it?" (2XwZWJ_Hnac [00:13:37]). Three equal ways to automate an elbow plate at 20% of the forearm: driver (locks the channel), Copy Rotation influence 0.2 Mix Add, Transformation 0..100 to 0..20 deg (2XwZWJ_Hnac [00:14:57] to [00:20:40]).
- Action constraint: AC_ action, keys 1 and 10, all Linear, fake user, unlinked from the rig, target local Y with max = measured control travel, constrained bones stay animatable; Limit Location with Affect Transform or a control dragged past its limit "goes dead" (2XwZWJ_Hnac [00:24:24], [00:40:08], [00:43:48]).
- Clean control-bone vertex groups: lock the DEF groups, delete unlocked (2XwZWJ_Hnac [00:05:29]).
- Ball rig: never rotate a scaled (stretched) bone, rotate a clean bone below the stretch; Euler for multi-turn spins; a quaternion counter-rotation (X/Y/Z = source, W = -source) cancels a parent rotation exactly; cancel inconsistencies are "a clear sign of a dependency loop" (HyD7dI0J_bA [00:17:50], [00:24:00], [00:19:37]; counter-rotation verified).
- Turret aim widget: Damped Track back to the turret creates a cycle; put the tracking on an MCH copy and show it through the control's Override Transform (Wayne AymxOGtJoew [00:38:28] to [00:42:21]).

## 8. Fundamentals as pitfalls (Wayne Dixon)

- Three roles: control, deform, mechanism; deform is on by default, turn it off everywhere else (AymxOGtJoew [00:06:17]). Name everything, never leave .001 (it breaks the L/R rhythm, batch renames and scripts) (AymxOGtJoew [00:09:48]).
- "If your rig is fit for purpose, you're done"; restrict only what breaks the rig or does nothing; do not add a floor constraint the animator must fight (AymxOGtJoew [00:10:53], [00:13:03]).
- Constraint stacks evaluate top to bottom; midpoint = Copy Location 1.0 then 0.5 ([00:22:16]; for n targets use 1, 1/2, 1/3 [added, verified]). A constrained bone cannot be animated: "Give it a parent, constrain the parent" ([00:28:31]). IK traps: constraint on the wrong bone, target inside the chain, chain length 0 climbs the hierarchy, IK Stretch 0.1 is "always fine" ([00:33:24] to [00:37:14]).

## 9. Disagreements and the deciding condition

| Topic                      | Positions                                                                                                                                                                                         | Decide by                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Face system                | Rigify modular face (CGDive, Ivan); hand-built face with action constraints (Demeter); hybrid bones + ribbon + shape keys + lattices (Rik); bones + ~16 shape keys + on-face controls (CGDive L3) | realism and number of characters vs a stylized hero that must stay on model; mocap (ARKit) vs hand animation |
| Twist                      | 1 twist + Preserve Volume (CGDive L2) vs 4 twists (L3)                                                                                                                                            | export or thick limbs: chains                                                                                |
| Colors                     | function colors (Rigify) vs left red/right blue (CGDive Rigify course, L1/L2) vs left blue/right red (CGDive L3)                                                                                  | ask the animator; keep one convention per rig                                                                |
| Finger controls            | master curls, scale to curl (CGDive) vs plain FK only (Demeter)                                                                                                                                   | crowd/fast animation vs feature animators posing fingers                                                     |
