- Several videos are vendor or sponsor content (Chaos, iToo, Arch Viz Artist sponsored by Chaos or
  plant vendors, Ross Plemya's farm). Settings are reliable; claims about speed or necessity are
  discounted.
- Most videos show 3ds Max 2020 to 2025 and Corona 9 to 13; no source shows 3ds Max 2027 itself.
