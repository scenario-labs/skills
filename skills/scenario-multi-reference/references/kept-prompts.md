# Kept prompts

Each prompt below produced a kept result. Pictures ran on a multi-reference image-edit model with the pictures as reference images in the numbered order; films ran on an image-to-video model with the picture as the first frame, 6 seconds, 720p, sound on. Spelling is American; the words are otherwise as run, including WHO, WHERE, STAR, and STAGE for the main subject and the place.

## Fridge drawing (3D animated film)

Picture:

```text
A still frame from a 3D animated family feature film. Each reference picture has exactly one job:
Image 1 (child's crayon drawing) = WHO. Turn this crayon monster into a soft, plush 3D animated character: round purple body, three uneven eyes, spiky green hair, tiny orange legs, big toothy smile. Keep its lopsided, hand-drawn charm.
Image 2 (backyard photo) = WHERE. Use this exact backyard: lawn, wooden fence, red hose, blue kiddie pool, swing set, lemon tree.
Image 3 (wooden mannequin) = POSE only. The monster leaps mid-air with both arms thrown up and one knee raised, exactly like the mannequin. Do not show the mannequin or any wood.
Image 4 (raccoon chef frame) = STYLE only. Copy its 3D render look, soft materials, warm bounce light and rich color. Do not include the raccoon, the kitchen, the apron or the pot.
Wide shot, the monster in the center above the lawn, afternoon sun, joyful.
```

Film:

```text
3D animated family film shot, static camera. The fluffy purple three-eyed monster bounces up and down on the backyard lawn, jumping again and again with joy: it squashes down on landing, springs high into the air with both arms thrown up, its spiky green hair and fur bouncing with each hop, tiny orange legs kicking. Big happy toothy grin, eyes blinking. Afternoon sun, the lemon tree leaves and swing sway gently in the breeze. Playful cartoon boing sounds, birds chirping.
```

## Gecko kaiju (ukiyo-e woodblock)

Picture:

```text
A traditional Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print, Edo-period printmaking. Each reference picture has exactly one job:
Image 3 (wave print) = STYLE. This is the most important job: everything, including the creature, is carved and printed like this wave print: flat color areas, bold black outlines, stylized curling linework, scales drawn as repeating carved patterns, wood grain and aged washi paper texture. Nothing photographic. Do not include the boats or mountain.
Image 5 (color swatch) = PALETTE, strictly. The whole print uses only these inks: deep indigo, Prussian blue, vermilion red and cream white (pale ochre allowed for paper tone). No green anywhere. The gecko is Prussian blue and indigo with vermilion spots and a cream belly.
Image 1 (gecko) = STAR. Use only the idea of a gecko: a colossal stylized gecko-kaiju, reinterpreted as a mythical creature like a dragon in old prints, with curling toes and a coiled tail, towering over the city and clinging to a building. Do not copy the photo's pose, lighting, colors or realism.
Image 2 (city crossing) = STAGE. The huge scramble crossing with a crowd and tall buildings, redrawn as woodblock architecture. The big billboards on the buildings each show a traditional woodblock motif: curling waves, swirling clouds, flying cranes, or a plain indigo panel with cream grid lines. No swoosh shapes, no split circles, no logos, no text anywhere.
Image 4 (storyboard sketch) = ANGLE only. Extreme worm's-eye view looking up from the street at the giant creature, strong converging lines. Do not include the lighthouse.
Wide composition. No text, no writing, no logos, no seals.
```

Film:

```text
An animated ukiyo-e woodblock print, everything stays flat printed ink in indigo, blue, vermilion and cream with paper texture. The giant gecko slowly turns its head down toward the crowd, blinks, and flicks out a long vermilion tongue; its coiled tail curls and uncurls. The crowd below sways and points up, the stylized clouds drift across the sky, the printed waves on the billboards roll. Static camera. Deep rumbling, crowd murmurs, a taiko drum hit.
```

## Jellyfish couture (fashion editorial)

Picture:

```text

Image 1 (woman portrait) = WHO. This exact woman: sharp black bob, freckles, confident neutral expression.
Image 2 (moon jellyfish) = WEAR. ```text

## Album cover (risograph poster)

Picture:

```text
A gig poster printed as a two-color risograph. Each reference picture has exactly one job:
Image 1 (musician) = WHO. This exact man: curly dark hair, mustache, denim jacket, bass guitar over his shoulder, as a halftone photo portrait.
Image 2 (Swiss poster) = LAYOUT only. Copy its strict grid: a huge bold sans-serif headline flush-left across the top, narrow text columns below, one big circle offset to the right, lots of empty paper. Do not copy the word MONDAY.
Image 3 (risograph leaves) = STYLE only. Copy the riso print texture: grain, coarse halftone dots, slight misregistration, uneven ink on textured paper. Do not include the leaves.
Image 4 (color swatch) = PALETTE. Only two inks: fluorescent hot pink and teal, overprinting to dark where they meet, on off-white paper. No yellow, no blue.
Text, exactly: the headline "LOW TIDE" in heavy bold uppercase sans-serif, and one small line "LIVE · 14 NOV" bottom-left. No extra words, no duplicate text. Portrait poster.
```

Film:

```text
An almost static risograph poster, locked-off camera. Everything stays exactly the same: the text "LOW TIDE" and "LIVE · 14 NOV", the grid, the pink circle, the bass guitar with its exact colors and position, his hands and jacket. Only the musician's face moves: he slowly breaks into a subtle smile and gives one wink to the viewer, with a tiny natural head tilt and breathing. Very minimal motion, halftone grain stays still. Quiet, a soft bass note.
```
