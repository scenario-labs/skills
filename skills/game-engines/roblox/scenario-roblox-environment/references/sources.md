# Sources: environment building and lighting

Every source this skill uses, with who made it, what it is good for and the timestamps worth
watching. Claims drawn from them, with agreements and disagreements:
[expert-notes.md](expert-notes.md). Videos were watched through their transcripts and checked
against on-screen values where the speech gave none.

## Plugins named (not installed by this skill)

- Sun Adjuster, Roblox asset 301432759 (DatBoiEle uses it to aim the sun). `Looks.aimSun` replaces
  it.
- Part to Terrain, built into the Terrain Editor (BONES, SmartyRBX). `TerrainKit.fromParts` replaces
  it.
