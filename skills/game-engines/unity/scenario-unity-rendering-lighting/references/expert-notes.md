# Expert notes: principles and judgment, by expert

Each line is attributed with its source id and timestamp (videos) or section (docs); full references in `sources.md`. [added] marks this skill's own additions, [observed] facts measured on this Mac in Unity 6000.3.21f1 on 2026-09-24 (`tests/code/unity-rendering-lighting/`, results in `archive/tests/unity-rendering-lighting/live_results.jsonl`). ## Pierre Yves Donzallaz (Unity senior lighting artist; Crysis, Ryse, GTA V, RDR2). PYD = yqCHiZrgKzs (HDRP, 2020); PYD22 = DlxuvvYZO4Q (HDRP Sponza, 2022)

- **Exposure is the foundation.** Wrong exposure forces nonsense intensities; natural and artificial light never balance (PYD [00:20:21], [00:28:42]). "If you want to light your Scene in a physically correct way, it is crucial that you use the right exposure." URP 6.3 translation: exposure is Fixed only (Color Adjustments Post Exposure, rps); choose it once per zone and never use it to rescue a light [added].
- **EV anchors** (EV100): sunny 14, overcast 10, sunset 8, very bright interior 6, night street 2, very dark interior 1, moonlit -1 (PYD [00:17:37] to [00:19:48]); PYD22 slide: moonless -2, moonlit 1, interior 4, low sun 8, cloudy 11, sunlit 14 (frame 00:03:59). Sun about 100,000 lux at about 5500 K; moon 0.5 lux at 4100 K; street lamp 2500 lm; headlight 2000 lm at 7000 K (PYD [00:34:51] to [00:36:57]). Fixed exposure default 0 is night and whites out daylight (use about 13.5, [00:21:56]).
- **Auto exposure limits come from fixed-exposure tests** of the brightest and darkest areas (13 and 6 EV in Sponza), then Automatic clamped to them (PYD22 [00:12:22] to [00:12:55]). URP has no auto exposure in 6.3: the URP equivalent is one Post Exposure per zone in local Volumes (PYD [00:21:25]: "Override Exposure in a volume per time of day or per zone").
- **Neutral inputs stay neutral:** shadows black for opaque casters, fog white, bloom tint white; color comes from lights and sky (PYD [00:39:38], [00:51:58], [01:03:39]).
- **Tonemapper:** ACES by default, Neutral for asset review because ACES adds contrast and desaturation (PYD [01:00:59], [01:01:29]). No tonemapping: clamped highlights, "terrible".
- **Volumes:** explicit priorities (two globals on the same parameter: last active wins, unpredictable, [00:15:33]); camera-specific effects (DoF, exposure, heavy fog) in a local volume on the camera ([00:16:03]).
- **Shadows:** shadow fade distance is the main shadow optimization; "very common for current-gen games to have a shadow distance under 30 meters" for local lights (2020, [00:43:15]); sun 200 m over a large plaza ([00:41:43]). Filtering quality over raw resolution ([00:40:09]). Slope-scale depth bias over normal bias in HDRP ([00:42:43]). Light leaks after a local light's shadow fades are a light-layer problem ([00:58:48]); URP 6.3: Rendering Layers on lights (EBL).
- **Reflection probes beat SSR;** a box probe around an enclosed area both captures it and shields it from the sky reflection ([00:46:37], [00:50:18]). PYD22: 13 probes for the Sponza atrium, surfaces under the same light share a probe, a probe restricted to the dark zone so it does not capture sun, box 4 x 4 x 25 m, blend 0.25 m ([00:09:07] to [00:10:14], frame 00:10:09).
- **The sky probe is the enemy of interiors:** without GI it tints interiors diffusely and specularly; weak artificial lights and blue-green materials are the sky leaking; dimming it with the Indirect Lighting Controller is "not the proper way" (PYD22 [00:03:22], [00:05:58], [00:08:17]). Mirror override (smoothness 1, black albedo) as a specular audit ([00:08:17]). [observed] Before a local reflection probe, the wooden table (smoothness 0.3) read gray-blue in the interior test (Table_Top blue minus red -0.07); after one box probe it read as wood (-0.39); the chrome ball went from +0.05 to -0.25 in one run (Editor captures of metals are unreliable, see the observations below).
- **Bake settings he used** (PYD22 frame 00:03:43): direct 8 to 16, indirect and environment 128 to 256, 4 bounces, 25 texels per unit (4 cm), padding 2, max 2048, directional, Normal Quality compression; bake 31 s lightmaps vs 8 s probe volumes vs 3 s legacy probes (frame 00:19:51).
- **Verdict:** lightmaps remain the cheapest on mobile; probe volumes "more interpolated" but scalable; mix techniques (PYD22 [00:20:08], [00:28:57]).

## Ciro Continisio (Unity EMEA lead evangelist). CC = hMnetI4-dNY (URP + GPU lightmapper, 2020)

- **Look through the player's eyes**, from the gameplay camera with textures on; artists chase acne nobody sees ([00:11:23], [00:58:39]).
- **Settings split by cost:** lightmap size, compression, directional mode change runtime memory (directional doubles the lightmap count) and must be agreed early per device with a technical owner; samples, bounces and filters change only bake time: "whether that's a nice bake or a bad bake the pictures are the same, the game doesn't care" ([00:35:51] to [00:36:56]). Iterate low, bake final rarely (for example nightly, [00:37:30]).
- **Shadow distance to what the view shows:** poles 10 m apart, 40 m leaves the last ones unshadowed, 600 m blurs everything, about 70 chosen ([00:07:28], [00:08:03]). With Subtractive, drop it very low so the whole map serves the few dynamic casters ([00:41:17]).
- **Texel economy:** Scale In Lightmap 0.2 for background, 1 for the play area ([00:17:30]); plants and small rocks Contribute GI but Receive GI from probes ([00:48:30]).
- **Filtering stays inside UV charts;** hand-authored lightmap UVs decide where blur may cross; A-Trous is edge-aware and stops bleed from texels under intersecting geometry that Gaussian spreads ([00:24:22] to [00:28:17]). Deciding condition vs Brackeys ("Gaussian in 90% of cases", VnG2gOKV9dw [00:13:31]): intersecting environment props favor A-Trous.
- **Fake thin emissives** with baked area lights just in front of them ([00:15:16]).
- **Probes where characters go** and where light changes; none in unreachable spots ([00:46:20] to [00:47:26]). Reflection probes by interest zone: exterior box plus interior box at Importance 2 ([00:54:35]).
- **Wrong normals spill light** from nowhere: check normals before settings ([00:58:05]).

## Unity lighting team, Unite 2024 (Chapelain, Grange, Llewelyn). APV24 = IpVuIZYFRg4 (Unity 6 Preview, URP demos)

- APV is "now the preferred choice for most projects" ([00:05:12]); Light Probe Groups survive for wearables and legacy hardware without compute ([00:08:31]). Deciding condition: compute support and texture-sampling budget.
- **Density is a budget you direct:** full 1 m range in interiors, a coarser override on open exteriors, background capped at 27 m; volumes merge, "create as many volumes as you want" ([00:11:54] to [00:14:25]). Defaults read on screen: min 1 m, max 81 m, Validity Threshold 0.75, Virtual Offset on, Dilation off (frame 00:11:01).
- **Leak ladder in their order:** Debug Probe Sampling (8 probes and weights per pixel), light Normal/View Bias (about 0.1 and 0.175), Interior/Exterior Rendering Layer masks and rebake (probes take the layer their rays hit most), Leak Reduction Mode Quality (up to 3 samples, never an invalid probe) on console and desktop, Performance acceptable on mobile after a look ([00:18:13] to [00:23:13]). Rendering Layers beat biases for clean interior/exterior splits because they change which probes are allowed.
- **Leak Reduction per platform:** Quality samples up to 3 times and never uses an invalid probe, "a slight performance overhead especially on low-end mobile"; Performance samples once and was "still usable" in the tent after a look ([00:22:36], [00:23:13]). In URP set it per tier in the tier's asset volume profile (ProbeVolumesOptions), Performance on phones, Quality on PC [added: the mechanism, tested in test 13].
- **Rendering Layer masks need Use Rendering Layers** on the URP asset (6.3 Manual, APV light leaks step 3); up to 4 masks; objects whose layers match no mask sample every valid probe; the demo assigned Interior and Exterior with a small editor script ([00:19:51], [00:20:58]).
- **SSAO and APV density substitute for each other:** SSAO restores the contact occlusion 1 m probes miss; without SSAO add one subdivision level in key areas (1 m to 0.3 m, 9 MB per scenario) ([00:25:24], frame 00:23:44).
- **Cost measured:** PS4 1080p lightmaps 0.203 ms, APV Performance 0.666 ms, Quality 0.830 ms, scenario blending 0.176 ms; memory Oasis APV 14.5 MB (9.5 MB positions once + 5 MB per scenario) vs 42.7 MB lightmaps (frames 00:07:47, 00:23:43). Deciding condition lightmaps vs APV: per-pixel GPU budget (lightmaps cheaper), memory and disk (APV smaller), UV authoring and iteration (APV), contact shadows (lightmaps; or SSAO / one more APV subdivision, [00:25:24]).
- **Sky occlusion vs lighting scenarios:** sky occlusion is one cheap bake that animates only skylight (a realtime sun loses its bounce); scenarios store full indirect incl. local lights; place one at every color or intensity change, closer where change is fast, about 8 for afternoon to midnight ([00:31:00] to [00:37:44]). Blend by real direct intensity, not by time (50 lux mid-way is about 75% blend, [00:44:57]). Compress intensities with one project-wide factor if auto exposure must travel the range (all lights / 30, [00:48:47]); "Using non-physically based values is also valid, as long as you know what you're doing" ([00:51:33]).

## Unity graphics product team, Unite 2025. U25 = K3-wPnhmDi4 (Unity 6.3 LTS)

- Renderer choice: Forward for budget mobile and most XR; Forward+ or Deferred+ for many realtime lights; HDRP for PC/console with ready-made advanced lighting ([00:19:48]). URP Deferred on iPhone 14 Pro used 60% less bandwidth than Built-in deferred (memoryless G-buffers, [00:29:22]): Deferred on tilers is a device-and-API question, not a blanket no.
- **Render Graph Viewer is the bandwidth audit:** blue line = merged native pass; Depth Texture inserts a copy and a final blit unless copied After Transparents; DoF makes depth non-memoryless ([00:30:26] to [00:34:40]). Depth prepass on PC and console, not on mobile ([00:36:45]).
- **Lighting basics first:** the sky feeds the ambient probe; in URP "you need to bake the ambient probe to make it change" ([00:43:52]); dynamic objects stay sky-tinted indoors until probes exist ([00:45:56]); small lightmap receivers make bakes take hours ([00:44:53]); Light Probe Groups break on large multi-part objects ([00:49:01]); "in any probe system in the world, there are light leaks" ([00:50:36]).
- "Be careful sometimes with ACES tone mapper because it can darken the scene quite a bit" ([00:54:50]).
- **Heat is the phone limit:** a phone runs at about 5 W with no active cooling and memory bandwidth is the main power consumer, so keep passes on tile ([00:26:45] to [00:28:20]). Verdicts on phones come from a thermal soak on the device, not the Editor [added: procedure owned by scenario-unity-mobile].
- **PSO hitches:** trace graphics states with GraphicsStateCollection in a development build, save them, then warm up progressively (the demo warmed 3 PSOs per step behind a progress bar); one collection per graphics API and platform; 6.1 extends warm-up to older APIs, 6.4 adds WebGPU ([00:17:11] to [00:19:48]). Shader Build Settings per build profile turn chosen keywords into dynamic branches for fast builds and keep multi_compile for old mobile GPUs ([00:15:36] to [00:16:37]).
- **GPU Resident Drawer** moves CPU-bound frames to the GPU (LEGO benchmark); Renderer Shader User Value keeps per-object variation in one SRP batch (4 draws vs 241) ([00:21:55] to [00:26:14]). [observed] Switching the interior grade from Neutral to ACES (plus contrast 14, saturation 18) moved the wall gray card from -0.58 to -2.0 EV; re-solving the zone exposure brought it back.

## Kyle Banks (solo developer, Farewell North). KB = 1agSNKuAfTM (URP, Unity 6.0)

- **Emotion first**, then the sun placed for it (diagonal through the key opening) ([00:03:25] to [00:03:55]).
- **Expect gray after baking;** fix it with ACES, contrast, saturation and Shadows Midtones Highlights ("my three go-tos"), each pushed too far then dialed back ([00:04:58], [00:12:36] to [00:14:08]).

## Unity documentation (6.3 Manual, URP Unity 6 e-book by Nik Lever, HDRP 2022 e-book, 2026 strategy)

- **Strategy (rps):** URP for anything new; HDRP in maintenance ("no new features are planned"), PC and console only (no mobile, web, Switch 1); Built-in deprecated in 6.5. URP 6.3 cannot: auto exposure, physical light units, SSR, SSGI, contact shadows, volumetric fog, IES, realtime area lights, transparent shadow casting (alpha clip only), shadows from additional directional lights. Brief a URP scene around these limits.
- **Shadows (ASSET, EBL):** Max Distance before resolution (40 m to 10 m let 1024 beat 2048); atlas spot 1, point 6 maps, up to 16, tiling 1 / 2x2 / 4x4; overflow shrinks every map and logs a warning; soft shadows Low 4 PCF taps (mobile), Medium 5x5 tent (desktop default), High 7x7; soft shadows high impact on tile-based mobile and untethered XR; Conservative Enclosing Sphere on for new projects.
- **Traps (ASSET):** Depth Priming makes custom opaque shaders invisible without DepthOnly and DepthNormals passes; unsupported with Deferred, MSAA, or TBDR mobile at runtime; Auto unsupported on Android, iOS, Apple TV. MSAA silently off with Opaque Texture on devices without StoreAndResolve. Store Actions = Store significantly increases bandwidth on tilers. Depth Texture After Transparents saves a store and reload on mobile. TAA and STP cannot run with MSAA, camera stacking or dynamic resolution; STP forces TAA, stays on at Render Scale 1.0, needs compute, never GLES. Forward+ ignores Per Object Limit and forces per-pixel lights.
- **Baking (TRB):** most defects trace to geometry and UVs (one-sided walls, intersecting props, overlapping UVs); denoisers need about 256 samples; fireflies 4K+ indirect; lightmaps are mipmapped (spacing that holds up close can leak far away); per-texture overrides on lightmaps are wiped by every rebake; Shadowmask stores 4 lights per texel; double-sided GI only on thin meshes; APV leak order: wall thickness, APV Options biases, Rendering Layer masks (max 4), smaller dilation and virtual offset, Probe Adjustment Volume (solves most, not all).
- **Stylized and low end (EBL):** Lit is PBR and best avoided on low-end mobile; Simple Lit is non-PBR Blinn-Phong suited to stylized low end; Baked Lit for static objects that never need realtime light. With soft shadows, above 1024 the stylized haunted room's shadows looked "too crisp". Mixed modes: Subtractive bakes the Mixed directional light's direct light into static geometry ("most suitable option for low-end hardware", static cannot shadow dynamic except through probes, tune Realtime Shadow Color); Baked Indirect "expensive, not ideal for mobile"; Shadowmask highest fidelity, most memory, mid to high end. GRD example: 3569 to 506 batches.
- **Shadowmask channels (TRB):** shadowmask textures share the lightmap layout and hold up to 4 lights per texel (RGBA); where more than 4 Mixed lights overlap the extras become fully baked and stay so on rebake; check with the Light Overlap draw mode.
- **Grading (ASSET, SBS):** HDR grading works before tonemapping, LDR after with limited range; under LDR a LUT applies after tonemapping (SBS frame 00:03:31); LUT sizes cannot be mixed, decide before grading. So every tier grades in one mode.
- **Distance Shadowmask on Forward+ (rps vs observed):** the 6.3 feature table lists URP Distance Shadowmask as "Forward rendering path only"; the same table lists MSAA the same way although the rendering path comparison gives Forward+ MSAA. Measured on 6000.3.21f1 (test 11): Distance Shadowmask renders identically on Forward+ and Forward. Deciding condition: trust the P13 measurement on the installed version; re-run it on Deferred or Deferred+ tiers (the Manual says to avoid Shadowmask with Deferred).
- **Ambient (EBL):** Unity 6 removed the SkyManager auto-bake: after a Skybox change, Generate Lighting; only Color and Gradient ambient update at runtime; APV sky occlusion needs the GPU lightmapper and Color/Gradient ambient.
- **Reflection (rps, EBL):** URP blends sky + 1 local probe (or 2 probes without sky), box projection yes, no per-probe resolution; one probe per lighting zone, box projection indoors.
- **Volumes (EBR, HDRP e-book):** Global 0, locals 1+; many volumes cost CPU; profile edits in Play mode persist on the asset; `SubmitRenderRequest` into a RenderTexture is the documented offscreen capture (the agent's eyes).

## This skill's observations [observed, 2026-09-24, Unity 6000.3.21f1, URP 17.3, Apple M5 Max, Metal]

- URP sun intensity 2 (what the unaided baseline picks) at Post Exposure 0: a sun-facing 18% gray card read +1.03 EV with no tonemapper (v0.2; sky ambient included, so sun 1 reads about 0 EV); the solved 0.98 read +0.16 EV under Neutral. v0.1 recorded +0.88 EV (and +0.19 EV at sun 1) believing there was no tonemapper, but the template's shared asset profile was applying Neutral (see the v0.2 observation below).
- `GameObject.CreatePrimitive(Plane)` and `(Quad)` have no uv2 (lightmap UVs); Cube, Sphere, Capsule, Cylinder do.
- Many URP asset fields have no public setter in C# (soft shadows, additional-light shadows, light probe system, mixed lighting, rendering layers, SH bands): edit them through SerializedObject; a fresh-process readback confirmed 58 fields.
- `GetComponent<T>() ?? AddComponent<T>()` fails in the Editor for a missing native component (fake null, MissingComponentException): use `== null`.
- `LightingSettings.autoGenerate` is obsolete (CS0618) in 6.3; `LightmapsMode.ToString()` prints "Single, Dual" for Directional (obsolete aliases).
- Bakes on the GPU lightmapper, headless: outdoor APV 28.5 s cold and 8.7 s warm, 14.3 MB of baking-set files (7.3 MB without the debug support data); interior lightmaps 6 to 15 s (GPU shared with other editors), one 512 x 512 BC6H set (color + directional) 0.67 MB GPU, plus 5.1 MB APV for dynamic objects; in Shadowmask mode a 512 x 512 shadowmask added 0.67 MB.
- The URP template's Mobile asset ships shadow bias depth 1 / normal 1. With 1024 px over 30 m and hard shadows, the realtime sun leaked through the wall-ceiling joint of the 0.3 m-walled room (a sawtooth line: 1,811 new near-white pixels vs PC); bias 0.1 / 0.5 left 25; a Shadowmask bake with the Mobile quality level on Shadowmask mode (static shadows baked, realtime map for dynamic casters) left 2 and smoothed the stair-stepped sun patch. Matches FFS [00:07:36] (bias too high = leaks) and CC's advice to bake what does not move.
- Depth Priming Forced with MSAA off made a forward-only custom shader invisible (the sphere showed the ground behind it); the same shader with DepthOnly and DepthNormals passes rendered; with MSAA 4x the forward-only sphere rendered again, because priming is not supported with MSAA. The trap therefore appears the day a tier moves from MSAA to TAA or STP.
- Editor batch captures rendered baked reflection probes black on metals on the Forward path (Mobile tier) every time, and on Forward+ in one of three runs, through both `SubmitRenderRequest` and `Camera.Render`, while `GetClosestReflectionProbes` returned the right probe and the mid-rough table did take the probe; 3 and 10 extra warm-up frames did not change it. macOS players on both paths (`_CLUSTER_LIGHT_LOOP` on and off) rendered the reflection correctly. Verify metal reflections in a player.
- A macOS player contains only the quality levels enabled for Standalone (`QualitySettings.names` = PC): a Mobile level cannot be selected there; switch the renderer of an included level to test a path.
- Under ACES + grade, the wall card moved 2.62 EV for a 1.49 EV Post Exposure change (slope 1.76): solve exposure iteratively (secant), not in one step.
- `ShaderUtil.GetShaderData` reads pass LightMode tags without a graphics device; `Shader.passCount` / `FindPassTagValue` missed URP Lit's DepthOnly and DepthNormals in a `-nographics` editor.
- Unity 6 gives a new, never-baked scene a default Lighting Data Asset: "no LDA" does not mean "not baked"; a bake writes a scene-owned LDA under `Assets/`.
- Once, a batch editor hung 9 minutes in shutdown after printing its `AGENT_RESULT` (ILPP unloading); the envelope was complete; the process was stopped by its PID.
- (v0.2) Distance Shadowmask on Forward+: with Max Distance 20 m, a static caster 11 m away and one 45 m away, the far shadow was present only with a shadowmask bake (1.89 stops vs 0.04 under Baked Indirect); rotating the sun 180 degrees without a rebake moved the near shadow (0.10 stop left) and kept the far one (1.89) on Forward+ and Forward alike; under Shadowmask mode both stayed. Keywords: SHADOWS_SHADOWMASK on for both modes, LIGHTMAP_SHADOW_MIXING only for Shadowmask.
- (v0.2) GPU Resident Drawer on 900 cubes sharing one mesh (3 materials), Editor Play mode: draw calls and batches 2,455 to 38, SetPass 37 to 27, main thread 3.4 to 1.2 ms (a second run: 2,439 to 22, 1.9 to 0.6 ms); the template ships BRG Variants KeepIfEntitiesGraphics and static batching on, which the report flags.
- (v0.2) Render Graph native passes on the Mobile tier (Metal): depth off 6, depth copy After Opaques 8 (opaque and skybox split from transparents), After Transparents 7 (merged again), Opaque Texture on 7 (split, plus Copy Color).

Giving each tier its own profile removed that tonemapper from scenes without their own grade: the outdoor "agent defaults" stage card moved from +0.88 EV (v0.1, under that Neutral tonemapper) to +1.03 EV (v0.2, no tonemapper; sun 2.0, so sun 1 reads about 0 EV in linear). Decide which profile owns the tonemapper.
- (v0.2) `APVLeakReductionMode.ValidityBased` is an [Obsolete] alias of Performance and `Enum.ToString()` prints it: read enum names skipping obsolete aliases.
- (v0.2) Keyword spaces: URP Lit and Simple Lit declare LIGHTMAP_ON, SHADOWS_SHADOWMASK, LIGHTMAP_SHADOW_MIXING, PROBE_VOLUMES_L1/L2; URP Unlit declares SHADOWS_SHADOWMASK only; a hand-written forward-only shader declares none.
