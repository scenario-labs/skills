# Shot list

Write the shot list after measuring the song and before generating any still. Every shot has a number, a duration
in whole beats, a shot size, a camera move and one action. One joke lands at the end.

## Template

| #   | Time       | Shot                                      | Camera       | Sound                   |
| --- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------- |
| 01  | 0 to 4 s   | Extreme close-up that sets the stakes     | Slow push-in | Narrator line 1         |
| ... |            |                                           |              |                         |
| 15  | 38 to 42 s | The punchline, on a face                  | Locked       | The song's last beat    |
| End | 42 to 47 s | Logo assembles on the title hit, Play Now | Locked       | Narrator says the title |

## Three structures that worked

**Action comeback (a cooking game).** Close-ups of the hero getting ready (oven dial, apron knot, a spoon slid into
the hair like a sword), two title cards, the gang arrives in the rain, a fast montage on the drop, the boss enters
as the music turns heavy, a standoff, and the boss only wants the special of the day.

**Epic fantasy (a stacking game).** A prophecy over a misty vista, the heroes arrive one by one and climb onto
each other, the music builds to one continuous crane shot to the top of the tower, then a tiny distraction (a red
laser dot) topples it in real time, and the heroes end up dizzy in the grass.

**Monster movie (a delivery game).** The delivery promise, the ride through the city (buses, a drawbridge, a car
wash), the music drops to silence as footsteps ripple a glass of water, a family frozen on the couch, a shadow on
the curtains, the doorbell, the peephole, and it is only the pizza, a little late.
